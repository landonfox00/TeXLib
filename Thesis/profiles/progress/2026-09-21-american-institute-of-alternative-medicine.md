# 2026-09-21 — american-institute-of-alternative-medicine

**blocked** — alphabetical queue. PR #263.

AIAM (Columbus, OH) has no thesis-bearing degree. Its highest credential is the
Master of Acupuncture, a clinical professional master's whose published
completion requirements are grades, the school's comprehensive exam, "at least
250 treatments and 50 different cases", attendance, conduct, payment and a time
limit. The rest of the school is practical nursing, registered nursing and
medical massage therapy.

What a later pass must not rediscover: AIAM's *School Catalog Winter 2026*
(`AIAM-Catalog_WI26.pdf`, 8,515 extracted lines) is open, downloads with a plain
`curl`, and contains the words **thesis, dissertation, capstone, margin, title
page and research project exactly zero times each**. This is not a documentation
gap — there is no written manuscript in any AIAM program to format. The catalog
is re-issued quarterly, so a later pass will find a newer filename under
`aiam.edu/wp-content/uploads/`; re-check only if AIAM adds a doctorate or a
research master's.
