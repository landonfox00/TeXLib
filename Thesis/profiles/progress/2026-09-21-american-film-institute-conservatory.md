# 2026-09-21 — american-film-institute-conservatory

**blocked** — alphabetical queue. PR #262.

AFI Conservatory grants the MFA in six disciplines and its FAQ does state a
requirement — "completion of a thesis or capstone project" — but nothing public
says what that artifact *is*, let alone how it is formatted. No margin, spacing,
title page, approval page, style manual or accessibility rule appears anywhere
on `conservatory.afi.com`.

What a later pass must not rediscover: the Policies and Procedures page exists
but publishes **no document**. It says only that "The AFI Conservatory policy
and procedures are available to Fellows via the Fellow Policies and Procedures
Handbook", which lives at `community.afi.com` behind a Fellow login. There is no
catalog PDF and no registrar format page. Finishing this profile means asking
Registrar@AFI.edu — and the first question to ask is whether a *typeset
manuscript* is filed at all, since at a film conservatory the thesis may well be
a film, in which case this institution belongs out of the worklist rather than
in a profile.
