# 2026-09-08 — agora-university

**blocked** — alphabetical fallback. Round of ten, institution 8 of 10.

Agora University (Burke VA; DEAC-accredited, SCHEV-certified, three colleges)
really does file a master's thesis and a doctoral dissertation. It publishes no
manuscript format for either — only length and process.

Things a later pass should not have to rediscover:

- **This is the "real thesis, no format" case, not "no thesis".** MTS 650 Master
  Thesis Writing: "Students will research, write and submit their 14,000-word
  Master thesis under the guidance of a supervisor from the Faculty", 10 hours
  of supervision per term, one term to complete. THD 801: "The dissertation must
  be at least 65,000 words with a maximum of 75,000 words, excluding
  bibliography", preceded by a progress defense on a 10,000-word excerpt and
  concluded by a viva before a three-member examination committee. Length,
  timeline, committee — never layout.
- **The governing document is the Student Handbook, and its URL is a hash.**
  `agora.edu/wp-content/uploads/2026/04/87b6e3825b30515aeaf78262891f939f.pdf` —
  112 pp, "Last Revised 04/2/26", "Handbook Effective Beginning: 4/2/26". It is
  linked from nowhere obvious; it was found by search. Expect the filename to
  change with each revision.
- **The `agora.edu/policies/` page is an extract of that handbook, not a
  separate source.** It quotes the handbook with page citations ("Agora
  University – Student Handbook Page 36-38") and carries no format rule.
- **All the keyword hits in the handbook are false positives.** Three `margin`
  matches: "Social Margins" in a faculty bio's dissertation title, and
  "marginalized voice" in two Alexandrian Church History course descriptions.
  Two `APA` matches are inside "capacities" and "Add". Zero for title page,
  signature page, double-spac, font, Turabian, Chicago.
- **The 2024 HTC handbook URL is dead.**
  `htc.agora.edu/wp-content/uploads/2024/01/Agora-University-Handbook-HTC-17-01-2024.pdf`
  now returns HTML, not a PDF (`pdftotext` fails on it). Don't chase it.
- Agora is a **distance** institution using Populi as its LMS; there is no
  library-side ETD deposit to mine for rules.
- No accessibility rule for a filed manuscript was found.
