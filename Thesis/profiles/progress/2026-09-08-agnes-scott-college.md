no manuscript format.

Things a later pass should not have to rediscover:

- **Every apparent "thesis" and "margin" hit is a false positive**, which is why
  a keyword count alone would mislead here. In the 133-page 2024-2025 Graduate
  Catalog, all ten `thesis` matches are inside *synthesis* / *hypothesis* except
  two transfer-credit lines, and those say the opposite of what a profile would
  want: "Transfer of thesis credits or credits for independent research is not
  allowed." All five `margin` matches are *marginal*, *marginalized* and "margin
  of admissibility". Zero for dissertation, title page, signature page,
  double-spac, font.
- **The culminating work is a digital portfolio, not a manuscript.** WDC-640 /
  WDC-699 Portfolio Capstone (0 credits), which Graduate Studies registers
  students into automatically in their final semester. The other graduate
  programs — Clinical Mental Health Counseling, Medical Sciences, Physician
  Assistant — end in coursework and clinical practice.
- **The catalog is a year stale and there is no newer one.** The URL pattern
  `agnesscott.edu/assets/documents/catalogs/grad-catalog-for-<YYYY-YYYY>.pdf`
  gives 200 for `2024-2025` and **404 for both `2025-2026` and `2026-2027`**
  (probed directly). Older editions use a different pattern,
  `<YYYY-YYYY>-graduate-catalog.pdf`. Nothing changes the verdict, but a later
  pass should check for a newer edition before re-running this.
- The `graduate-studies/index.html` page links no catalog or handbook at all;
  the catalog PDFs are only reachable by search or by guessing the asset path.
- No accessibility rule for a filed manuscript was found.
