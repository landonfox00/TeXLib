# 2026-09-21 — american-academy-of-health-and-wellness

**blocked** — alphabetical queue. PR #257.

AAHW (Roseville, MN; IPEDS lists it under the old name, the site brands as
American Academy of Health and Wellness) does award a doctorate, the DAOM, and
its final written work is a **capstone research project** with an oral defence —
4 credits in the final year, fed by a "Clinical Research Paper Writing" course.
So the institution is correctly in the worklist. What it does not publish is any
*format* for that document: no margin, spacing, title-page, approval-page, style
manual or accessibility rule appears anywhere on the public site.

What a later pass must not rediscover: AAHW publishes **no catalog PDF at all**.
`student-policies` states the AAHW Student Handbook and Clinic Handbook are
"distributed to all entering students and … available upon request" — i.e. the
document that would carry any filing rule is deliberately not on the web. The
only PDF linked anywhere under `acupunctureschoolusa.com/wp-content/uploads/pdf/`
is the annual campus safety report. `aahw.edu` is a redirect shell with no
document links of its own. Finishing this profile means requesting the handbook.
