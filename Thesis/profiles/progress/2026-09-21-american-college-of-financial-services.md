# 2026-09-21 — american-college-of-financial-services

**blocked** — alphabetical queue. PR #260.

The American College of Financial Services (King of Prussia, PA) has no
thesis-bearing degree to write a profile for. Its own Legacy Programs page
states in as many words that "The part-time PhD in Financial and Retirement
Planning began in July 2013 and was phased out in April 2018"; six doctorates
were awarded. The only graduate degree now offered is the MSFP — ten courses
plus an in-person residency, whose final element is *MSFP 650 Capstone Case
Development*, a residency course rather than a filed document. Everything else
the College offers is a professional designation (CLU, ChFC, RICP, CFP tracks),
not a degree.

What a later pass must not rediscover: **IPEDS HD2023 still flags this school
`doctoral=1`**, which is why the queue served it; that flag is stale by the
institution's own account. Nothing on theamericancollege.edu is behind a login
here — the site publishes no academic catalog or student handbook at all, so
even if the sunset PhD's dissertation format once existed in writing, it is not
on the public web. Re-check only if the College starts a new doctorate.
