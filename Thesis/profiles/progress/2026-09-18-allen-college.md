# 2026-09-18 — allen-college

**blocked** — alphabetical fallback. PR #248. Round of ten, institution 3 of 10.

Allen College (UnityPoint Health, Waterloo, IA) is a health-sciences college
whose doctorate is the DNP. It publishes no manuscript format for any
graduate work. The DNP ends in a defended "DNP clinical scholarship project",
not a dissertation, and the college's graduate policies say so without
prescribing its form.

What was read, and what it says:

- **SOP 2-A-900-06, Academic Progression – Graduate Nursing Programs**
  (September 2025): students enrol in NU800 "until they have successfully
  defended their DNP clinical scholarship project" and must file with the
  Allen College IRB (ACIRB). No format.
- **SOP 2-G-800-02, Graduation Policy**: "Complete DNP clinical scholarship
  project", and "Graduate students must have completed any end-of-program
  project or thesis by a date established each semester by the College's
  registrar". No format.
- The DNP program page, the Graduation Information page, the Library page and
  the Policy Library index: no thesis/project formatting document is linked.

A later pass should look for a DNP handbook if one is ever posted publicly;
none was found by site-restricted search on the access date. Search results
for this institution are dominated by Allen *School* of Health Sciences and
Allen *Community* College — different institutions. No accessibility rule for
a filed manuscript was found.
