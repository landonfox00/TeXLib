# 2026-09-17 — albany-medical-college

**blocked** — alphabetical fallback. Round of ten, institution 2 of 10.

Albany Medical College really does file M.S. and Ph.D. theses in the basic
sciences, and the format exists — it just is not public. The governing public
document, the **Graduate Studies Program Policy Handbook** ("revised 9-2022",
37 pp, linked from `amc.edu/institutional-policies/graduate-studies-program-rules/`
as `wp-content/uploads/sites/3/2024/01/Policy-Handbook-revised-9-2022-211.pdf`),
§12.2.5 Thesis Defense, says:

- "Students should consult the Instructions for Submission of the Ph.D. Thesis
  available in the Graduate Studies Program Office and work with their thesis
  committee on the format of the thesis."
- Final submission is a PDF "(including the signed approval page)" to the GSP
  and to the department, plus a PDF and "an acid-free paper copy (meeting the
  AMC requirements for thesis submission)" to the Schaffer Library.

So a **signed approval page is required**, but its wording, and every margin
and spacing rule, sit in an office handout that was not found online.

A later pass should not have to rediscover:

- Searches for the "Instructions for Submission" return nothing; the Schaffer
  Library's Research & Publishing page covers journal publishing, not theses.
- The old `amc.edu/academic/graduatestudies/*.cfm` URLs redirect to
  `amc.edu/education/basic-science-ms-phd/`.
- Search engines conflate AMC with the University at Albany (SUNY), which has
  its own profile slug — do not borrow its rules.
- The handbook contains no accessibility rule for a filed thesis (its one
  "accessibility" hit is about journal availability).

Retry if the Graduate Studies Program publishes the instructions.
