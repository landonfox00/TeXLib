# 2026-09-10 — university-of-cincinnati-main-campus

**profile** — HERD rank 37. PR #178.

Set: geometry, spacing, the title page from the Graduate College's own sample
Doctoral title page, `\thesisnoapprovalpage`, and four accessibility items.
Nothing is left to the neutral fallback.

**The important finding is about status, not figures.** UC deliberately does not
set binding format requirements centrally, and says so in the first line of its
own guidelines: "While specific formatting requirements are determined by your
committee and/or department, the following information provides 'best practice'
guidelines", and again "You and your committee should agree on formatting
requirements for the ETD." So the 1in margins and double spacing here are the
Graduate College's *recommendation*. They are set anyway, because they are what
UC publishes and they coincide with the class's neutral defaults — the effect is
to record a citation, not to move anyone's margins. Do not upgrade the header's
hedging into a claim that UC requires them.

What UC *does* require centrally is narrower: the title page's content list, the
fifteen-entry page order, and "All fonts must be embedded in your pdf."

**Every accessibility item at UC is a should or a recommendation** — "students …
**should** make their ETDs accessible", and the list is headed "**Recommended**
Minimum Guidelines for Digitally Accessible PDF Files in the OhioLINK ETD
Center". So neither `\thesisrequiretagging` nor
`\thesisrequiredocumentlanguage` is set, even though UC's list names searchable
text and a specified language. Recorded verbatim instead.

**The approval page is generated by the Graduate College, not by the author.**
The Required Page Order has fifteen entries and none is an approval page; what
exists is the **Document of Defense**, "a version of the Committee Approval
Form … includes printed names", produced through the Graduation Checklist, and
UC states "The practice above has been established and implemented on behalf of
the students; no action is required." Printed names, not signatures; approval is
recorded in the ETD system by the chair.

**An ambiguity left for the reader, not resolved.** The required-content list
names both "Author name and date" and "Previous degrees", and the sample prints
"M.A. University of Cincinnati" then "June 2008" on consecutive lines. That date
reads either as the previous degree's or as the document's. The profile emits
`\ucpriordegrees` then `\thesis@date` (the document's reading) and documents how
to get the other one. Do not quietly pick a side later.

Also unusual for this directory: **the title is not uppercased** — "Do not use
all capital letters for your title."
