# 2026-09-08 — adams-state-university

**blocked** — alphabetical fallback (the HERD list is exhausted). Round of ten,
institution 1 of 10.

Adams State grants doctorates but publishes no university-wide manuscript
format. The Office of Graduate Studies page, the Graduate Academic Policies
index and the catalog were all read and none states a margin, spacing,
title-page or approval-page rule.

Things a later pass should not have to rediscover:

- **There IS a real formatting document on adams.edu, and it is out of scope.**
  Appendix A ("Dissertation Formatting Instructions") of the *School of
  Counselor Education, Doctor of Philosophy, Counselor Education & Supervision
  Dissertation Manual 2026-2027* — 40 pp, linked from
  `/academics/graduate/counselor-education/doctorate/dissertationhandbook/`,
  served from Google Drive — prescribes margins, 2in chapter openings, font,
  spacing, a title page and a signature page in detail. It is a *program*
  document, which RESEARCHING.md excludes as a source. Do not promote it to a
  university profile just because it is the only thing there.
- **That manual contradicts itself on the left margin.** Its
  LIBRARY/PROQUEST section says "Margin: Left: 1"; Right: 1"; Top and Bottom:
  1"", while Appendix A says "Format the left margin 1 1/2 inches to accommodate
  binding (if desired) on the entire dissertation". Even if someone later
  decides a program manual is admissible for this institution, that margin has
  to be resolved with the school, not picked.
- The manual would otherwise support `\thesissetchapteropening{2in}`: "Start the
  first page of each chapter and new section 2" from the top of the page."
- **Signatures are still physical**: "Every copy must have original signatures",
  and the signed approval page goes inside the uploaded ProQuest manuscript.
- No accessibility rule for the document was found. The manual has an
  "ACCESSIBILITY AND ACCOMMODATIONS" section, but it is about student
  disability accommodations, not about the filed PDF.
- The catalog is Acalog (`adams.catalog.acalog.com`); its advanced-search URL
  returns an empty body to WebFetch. Use the PDF catalog instead if a later pass
  needs it.
