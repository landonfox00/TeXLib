# 2026-09-11 — virginia-polytechnic-institute-and-state-university

**profile** — HERD rank 40. PR #186.

Set: the title page (from the Graduate School's own MS Word front-matter
template), `\thesisnoapprovalpage`, `\thesisrequiretagging`, and eight quoted
accessibility rules. Left neutral: **geometry and spacing**, which is the
substantive gap.

Things a later pass must not rediscover:

- **Virginia Tech publishes no margin or spacing figure.** The Formatting
  page's only statement is circular — "formatted to fit within the margins
  acceptable by the Graduate School" — and no figure appears on the Formatting,
  Accessibility or Policies pages. Do not go looking for prose that is not
  there; read the figures out of the Graduate School's front-matter templates
  (VTechWorks handles 10919/116253 and 10919/73226, or the Overleaf template).
- **Why a LibGuide is cited here:** `etd.vt.edu/guidelines/format.html`, the
  Graduate School's own ETD host, answers **301** to
  `guides.lib.vt.edu/ETDguide`. The redirect is what makes the guide the
  Graduate School's designated requirements page; the title page itself comes
  from `graduateschool.vt.edu` regardless.
- **The title page is the approval instrument.** The Templates box offers one
  title page "for unanimous approval" and a different one to use "If one or
  more of your committee members do not approve your work" (split approval).
  The profile implements the unanimous page only; the split-approval variant is
  unimplemented and a student in that situation must not use it.
- Both guide pages carry "Last Updated: Aug 10, 2026" — current.
- `\thesisrequiredocumentlanguage` deliberately not set: the page never
  mentions document language. No PDF standard is named either.
