spacing, chapter opening and the title page — Alabama State publishes none of

For a later pass, so it is not rediscovered:

- Both university-level documents were read in full — the 4-page ETD guidelines
  ("rev. Fall 2021") and the 110-page Graduate Bulletin. Neither states a
  margin, spacing, typeface or title-page layout. The Bulletin sends candidates
  to program handbooks by name ("The Graduate Student Handbook, Biological
  Sciences"). This is the **same shape as Northeastern**: no university-wide
  format, and the same open question about per-program profiles.

  ProQuest's: "ProQuest asks that signature pages NOT be included in the
  electronic document." ASU prints it in its own guidelines and separately
  requires four to six signed paper copies filed with the Graduate School, the
  advisor, the college and the department. Signatures are still collected — just
  not inside the PDF. The provenance is quoted in the profile so a later pass
  can weigh it.
- The only paper/format rule either document gives ("white 20-24 lb.,
  acid-free/archival, 8 1/2 x 11, watermarked") applies to **optional bound
  copies**, not to the filed PDF.
- No accessibility requirement at either level; the Bulletin's two "accessible"
  hits are about buildings.
