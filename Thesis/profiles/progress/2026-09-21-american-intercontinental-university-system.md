# 2026-09-21 — american-intercontinental-university-system

**blocked** — alphabetical queue. PR #264.

Two separate reasons, either of which alone would block this slug.

**AIU awards no doctorate and no thesis-bearing master's.** Its 2026 catalog
(SmartCatalog) offers associate, bachelor's and master's degrees plus
certificates; the master's are "45 credits with graduate-level research and
critical inquiry integrated into course requirements", and the terminal element
is a **capstone course**, defined as "a culminating academic experience…
compreh[ensive] assessment of the program learning outcomes" — a course, not a
filed manuscript. Graduation requirements are a CGPA and departmental
clearances. No margin, spacing, title page, approval page, style manual or
accessibility rule anywhere.

**The slug is a system office, not the degree-granting body.** `aius.education`
is the American InterContinental University System: AIU, **California Southern
University** and **Trident University International**, each with its own
catalog. The `doctoral=1` flag IPEDS carries for unitid 445027 reflects
CalSouthern's doctorates, and CalSouthern is its own IPEDS institution that the
alphabetical queue will serve separately under `california-southern-university`.
A single profile at this slug would have to pick one member's rules and label
them "American InterContinental University System", which is exactly the kind of
misattribution RESEARCHING.md exists to prevent.

What a later pass must not rediscover: `aiuniv.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/…`
is a **404** — the year must be spelled out, e.g.
`…/en/2026/catalog/programs-and-courses/graduation-requirements`. Do the
doctorate research under CalSouthern's own slug, not here.
