# 2026-09-18 — alverno-college

**blocked** — alphabetical fallback. PR #254. Round of ten, institution 9 of 10.

Alverno College (Milwaukee, WI) grants an Ed.D. with a dissertation but
publishes no manuscript format for it or for any other graduate work.

What was read:

- **Ed.D. Program Handbook** (`alverno.edu/files/galleries/EdD_Program_Handbook.pdf`,
  32 pp): course descriptions and assessment; the only style reference is that
  students in EDD 801 "will develop comfort and fluency with APA style and
  formatting". No margin, spacing, title-page or approval-page rule.
- **Catalog, Education Doctorate Programs** (`catalog.alverno.edu/laps/ed/edd/`):
  dissertation seminars and a "Dissertation Proposal Defense Template" for
  the *presentation*, whose only format mention is a rubric row on
  "conventions (usage, spelling, punctuation, capitalization, sentence
  structure, format, citing and documenting sources)".
- **Catalog site search** (CourseLeaf, `catalog.alverno.edu/search/?search=…`,
  which works from a script): "dissertation format", "thesis format" and
  "margins" return nothing about a filed manuscript. The "margins" hits are
  "marginalized".

Alverno keeps a digital collection of Ed.D. dissertations
(`alverno.omeka.net/collections/show/4`); a later pass could look there for a
shared layout, but a collection of past documents is not a requirement and
cannot be a source. No accessibility rule for a filed manuscript was found.
