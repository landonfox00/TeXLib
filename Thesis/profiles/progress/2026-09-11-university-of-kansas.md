# 2026-09-11 — university-of-kansas

**profile** — HERD rank 46. PR #192.

Set from the Office of Graduate Studies' 2025 ETD Guidelines and the live
`graduate.ku.edu/formatting` page: geometry (1in minimum), double spacing,
`\thesisrequiretagging`, five quoted accessibility rules, and the two required
pages — Title and **Acceptance**.

Things a later pass must not rediscover:

- **The PDF is not the whole rule.** The 2025 ETD Guidelines predate KU's
  accessibility requirement, which exists only on `graduate.ku.edu/formatting`
  and starts with Spring 2026 graduates. Read both.
- **KU's accessibility requirement is a numeric score from a named tool:** "a
  YuJa Panorama Accessibility Report with a score of 85 or higher", uploaded as
  the **Word/LaTeX source, not a PDF**, before converting for ProQuest. Its
  April 2026 / May 15 2026 dates are KU's own — do not reconcile against
  neighbouring profiles.
- **KU publishes no specimen and no prescribed wording** for either page — only
  an element list, role rules, signing rules and the page number. So the
  arrangement and the connective phrases in this profile are the class's, not
  KU's, and the file says so. This is a genuinely weaker basis than the other
  profiles in this round, which all had a specimen.
- **The Acceptance Page must show page number 2** — "The Acceptance page number
  must be 2. This number should appear on the page." So this profile's approval
  page deliberately does **not** load `nopagenumber` geometry and does not set
  `\thispagestyle{empty}`; the document must have numbering live and arrange for
  it to be page 2.
- **Signing splits between media:** neither the Title Page nor the Acceptance
  Page may be signed in the electronic submission, but "students must submit the
  signed copy of the Acceptance Page, bearing signatures of their chair(s), to
  their schools in hard copy."
- **Roles ARE printed beside committee names here** (the supervisor "must be
  designated on the Title Page as Chair") — the opposite of Georgia and Miami in
  this same round.
- The acceptance page names the **chair only**; the whole committee belongs on
  the title page.
- KU's guidelines reuse text from the University of Chicago's dissertation
  requirements, with permission — an odd cross-link, and unrelated to the fact
  that Chicago itself was blocked this round.
