# 2026-09-15 — northeastern-university

**profile** (partial, deliberately) — HERD rank 99. PR #228.

Set: the institution name and `\thesisnoapprovalpage`. Left neutral: geometry,
spacing, chapter opening and the title page — not because they could not be
found, but because **Northeastern has no university-wide filing format**. The
library states it: "Each college at Northeastern University maintains its own
individual requirements for the preparation of theses and dissertations."

For a later pass, so it is not rediscovered:

- Three colleges were read. **Engineering** publishes complete figures (left
  1.5in, others 1in; 1.5 or double spacing; 10–12pt; major headings 2in from the
  top; page numbers upper right). **CSSH** publishes none and points at
  ProQuest's guide plus a format-review appointment. **College of Science**'s
  2017–18 guide is procedural and states no margin. All three figures are
  recorded in the profile header, ready to paste into a preamble — do not
  promote Engineering's to the institution level.
- `\thesisnoapprovalpage` is well supported: COE says "Do not include signature
  page in the final ETD submission", and CSSH and COS both route signatures to a
  separate **NU Graduate Approval Record**. No source read requires an approval
  page inside the manuscript.
- No accessibility requirement exists at any level; five documents searched.
- COE's style guide cover reads "Revised 2016" — the oldest live source in this
  round. Its submission procedures are "Revised: 2021".
- The open question for a person: whether per-college profiles
  (`northeastern-university-coe`, …) should exist. That is a naming-scheme
  change, so the routine did not make it.
