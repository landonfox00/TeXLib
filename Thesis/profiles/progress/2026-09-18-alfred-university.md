# 2026-09-18 — alfred-university

**blocked** — alphabetical fallback. PR #246. Round of ten, institution 1 of 10.

Alfred University has no central graduate school and publishes no
university-wide manuscript format. The Graduate Academic Regulations
(`my.alfred.edu/academic-regulations/graduate.cfm`) set degree requirements —
a doctoral thesis for the engineering PhD, a dissertation for the PsyD, a
thesis for the MS and MFA — but no margin, spacing, title-page or
approval-page rule.

Things a later pass should not have to rediscover:

- **The only format rules are the School of Engineering's, and they are out of
  scope.** The "Engineering Students Thesis Format Checklist" (a .docx, contact
  Matt Lake, SoE) is served from the LIBRARY guide
  `libraries.alfred.edu/soe-thesis/graduate` (`ld.php?content_id=73560682`),
  alongside an "SOE Thesis Template", a review form and deposit forms. It is a
  school document hosted on a library guide; RESEARCHING.md admits neither.
  For the record, it says: "Margins: 1.5” on left; other margins are 1”",
  "Line Spacing: 1.5 for body, single space within references and non-textual
  (figure) descriptions", title and signature pages "counted (I, ii) but not
  paginated", ACS references. It governs engineering students only; a PsyD or
  MFA filer is not bound by it.
- **The SoE Graduate Program Manual** (`my.alfred.edu/engineering/_docs/graduate-manual.pdf`,
  "2022-2023, Last Updated: August 2021") has no format section. It says the
  thesis is "reviewed for formatting" by the Administrative Assistant of the
  Graduate Programs and that non-voting committee members' names "will appear
  on the thesis signature page". Its 1in / single-spaced figures are for the
  *qualifying-exam proposal*, not the thesis — do not mine them.
- Engineering theses are still filed on paper: three printed copies on thesis
  paper plus a PDF for the AURA repository, per the library guide.
- No accessibility rule for a filed manuscript was found anywhere.
