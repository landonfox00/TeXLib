# 2026-09-18 — alma-college

**blocked** — alphabetical fallback. PR #252. Round of ten, institution 7 of 10.

Alma College (MI) is a master's-only institution with two graduate programs,
the low-residency MFA in Creative Writing and an online MA in Special
Education (`alma.edu/academics/programs/graduate-programs/`). It has no
graduate school and publishes no thesis filing format.

Things a later pass should not have to rediscover:

- **The MFA Student Handbook** (`alma.edu/wp-content/uploads/2023/08/MFA-STUDENT-HANDBOOK.pdf`,
  34 pp, titled "UPDATED Sept", created 2023-08-31) is a program document and
  in any case sets no filing format. Degree requirements include "a critical
  thesis (20 pages)" and "a creative thesis manuscript (125 pages for prose,
  45 pages for poetry)". Its only layout rule is for monthly PACKETS: "typed,
  double-spaced with 1-inch margins, 12-point serif font (e.g. Times New
  Roman)", plus MLA for the annotated bibliography. Do not mine that as a
  thesis margin.
- Approval of the creative thesis is by the mentor, and the final manuscript
  "must be approved and signed by the MFA director"; no signature page layout
  is given.
- The MA in Special Education page names no thesis.
- No accessibility rule for a filed manuscript was found.
