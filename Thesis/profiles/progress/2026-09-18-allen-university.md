Allen University (Columbia, SC) publishes no university-wide manuscript
format. Its graduate work is spread across programs with separate rules or
none: an MS in Biology with BIO 598/599 "Thesis Research", an MA in Religious
Studies ending in "RES 789 Master Thesis", MBA/sport-management degrees, and
the Doctor of Ministry at Dickerson-Green Theological Seminary (DGTS), the
university's seminary. The **Academic Catalog 2026-2028**
(`allenuniversity.edu/s/Catalog2026-2028V2-1.pdf`, 8,973 lines of text)
describes those courses but states no margin, spacing, title-page or
signature-page rule anywhere.
