# 2026-09-08 — adler-university

**blocked** — alphabetical fallback. Round of ten, institution 4 of 10.

Adler University (Chicago / Online / Vancouver) runs a real, centralised format
review and still publishes no university-wide manuscript format. The review
defers to APA plus departmental style guides, and each program carries its own
handbook and template.

Things a later pass should not have to rediscover:

- **Adler's format authority is the Library, and that is NOT the usual
  "library guide" exclusion.** `library.adler.edu` hosts the Format Review
  service: "The Writing Center will assign a format reviewer and send them your
  files", and review happens "after the defence of your dissertation or thesis".
  It was read on the merits rather than dismissed. It still yields nothing,
  because it explicitly defers: reviewers check "adherence to APA style and to
  departmental style guidelines". Adler naming APA is not Adler stating a
  margin.
- **Fourteen program documents, no university-wide one.** The Handbooks by
  Program page (`library.adler.edu/formatreview/handbooks`) lists handbooks
  and/or templates for PhDAT, DCES, CFT, Chicago PsyD and CIP MA (Chicago); the
  Online Campus PhD; and MACP, MAIOP and Vancouver PsyD (Vancouver). None is
  marked as applying to all programs. If a later pass wants a profile here it
  has to be a per-program decision, which this scheme has no slug for.
- The CIP guide's own scope note shows how narrow these are: "for MA students
  who chose to complete a thesis for the following Chicago campus programs:
  CMHC, MACR, MACF/FMHL, MASH/MSHP".
- Handbook and template downloads are LibGuides asset URLs of the form
  `library.adler.edu/ld.php?content_id=<id>`; the ids are listed on the
  handbooks page and are stable enough to fetch directly.
- No accessibility rule for a filed manuscript was found on any of the central
  pages.
