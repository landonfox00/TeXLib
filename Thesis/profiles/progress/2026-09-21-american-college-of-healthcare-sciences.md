# 2026-09-21 — american-college-of-healthcare-sciences

**blocked** — alphabetical queue. PR #261.

ACHS (Portland, OR) has a genuine doctoral dissertation — DSC 700 Dissertation
Seminar → DSC 701 → DSC 702, a dissertation committee with a chair, IRB review,
a Dissertation Proposal and Approval Form, and a final oral defence — and the
*Program Catalog 2025-26* prescribes the **five-chapter structure** explicitly
(Introduction, Literature Review, Methodology, Results, Discussion). Like ACAOM
earlier in this round, that is structure, not format: the catalog names no
margin, spacing, typeface, title page, approval page, or accessibility rule.

What a later pass must not rediscover:

* The catalog is a single open PDF at
  `https://files.achs.edu/mediabank/achs_program_catalog.pdf` — plain `curl`,
  9,140 lines of clean `pdftotext -layout`. It was searched for `margin`,
  `double spac`, `title page`, `font`, `APA`, `signature`, `accessib`, `WCAG`
  and `formatting`; nothing relevant.
* **APA 7th appears in the catalog only as a library support service** ("APA and
  Citations Assistance… downloadable APA and references cheat sheets"), not as a
  filing requirement for the dissertation. Do not promote that into
  a style rule.
* The "Dual Capstone Policy" (p. 202) is about how many capstones a dual-degree
  student writes, not how one is formatted.
* CAP 501, the alumni theses page and the ACHS blog posts were all read and carry
  nothing either.
