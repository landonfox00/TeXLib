% A NOTE ON THE SOURCE, because RESEARCHING.md rules out library guides.
% At BU the Libraries ARE the filing authority: "Submitting your thesis or
% dissertation to Boston University Libraries is the last step to fulfill at the
% University before you graduate and are awarded your degree", and the guide
% says of itself "This guide takes precedence should there be any conflict in
% formatting or standard requirements." It is the institution's own filing
% requirements document that happens to be hosted on LibGuides, not a librarian's
% advice about someone else's rules. BU has no central graduate school; the
% thirteen schools and colleges file through this one guide.
%
% VERIFIED and set here: geometry, spacing, title page, readers' approval page,
% and the (absent) accessibility requirement.
% NOT set: nothing is left to the neutral fallback.

\thesisinstitution{Boston University}

% "Top margin: 1.5 inches to first line of text or heading / Left margin: 1.5
% inches / Right margin: 1 inch. You may choose to justify the right margin. /
% Bottom margin: at least 1 inch from the bottom of the page".
%
% "Use these margins at all times. Do not exceed the right hand 1-inch margin.
% Extra wide margins are not allowed. Only the page number lies outside these
% margins."
%
% Note the TOP margin is 1.5in on every page, not a chapter exception, which is
% why \thesissetchapteropening is not called: BU states one figure and applies
% it "at all times".
\thesissetgeometry{left=1.5in, right=1in, top=1.5in, bottom=1in}

% "Double space all textual material and preliminary pages, including table of
% contents. Footnotes, figure and table captions, and long quotations may be
% single spaced. The bibliography may be single-spaced, with one extra space
% between each citation." The permitted single-spaced items are per-element.
\thesissetspacing{double}

% Type size: "Use 12-point font for the text, all headings, and subheadings.
% Titles or major headings may be in all upper case letters, bold, italics, or
% underlined, but they cannot be larger than 12-point." The class already loads
% report at 12pt, so nothing is needed here -- but note the second clause: a
% profile or document must not enlarge headings past 12pt at BU.
%
% Typeface: "Use standard font types such as Arial, Cambria, Garamond, Palatino
% Linotype, Times New Roman, Verdana etc. ... Avoid Calibri". The list is open
% ("such as ... etc.") and names no requirement, so this profile sets no face.
% BU also asks that all fonts be embedded, which this class does.

% --- Accessibility -----------------------------------------------------------
% Read on the access date: the full guide PDF (searched for "accessib", "alt
% text", "alternative text", "tagged", "WCAG", "PDF/A", "screen reader", "508",
% "Title II" -- the only hits are about open ACCESS, not accessibility), the ETD
% checklist, and the guide's Step 2 page. BU asks two things of the PDF file and
% neither is an accessibility rule:
\thesisaccessibilityrequirement{Not published}
	{No BU accessibility requirement for a filed thesis or dissertation was found
	 on the access date, in the Guide for Writers of Theses \& Dissertations, the
	 ETD checklist, or the Libraries' submission pages. This is "BU publishes
	 none", not "nobody looked".}
\thesisaccessibilityrequirement{PDF settings}
	{"Your PDF must have all fonts embedded and security settings showing 'No
	 security.'" and "Your PDF should be set to Document Restrictions Summary ->
	 Everything allowed." These are reuse and preservation rules rather than
	 accessibility ones, but they are the only constraints BU places on the file,
	 and a restricted PDF is one an assistive tool cannot open.}

% --- Author-facing fields this profile adds ----------------------------------
% BU's title page carries two things the class does not model: the OFFICIAL
% SCHOOL NAME, which must be taken from a fixed list, and the author's prior
% degrees. Both default to visible placeholders rather than vanishing, because
% both are required.
%
% The list, verbatim from "LIST OF OFFICIAL SCHOOL NAMES" in the samples:
%   COLLEGE OF COMMUNICATION
%   COLLEGE OF ENGINEERING
%   COLLEGE OF FINE ARTS
%   GRADUATE SCHOOL OF ARTS AND SCIENCES
%   HENRY M. GOLDMAN SCHOOL OF DENTAL MEDICINE
%   METROPOLITAN COLLEGE
%   QUESTROM SCHOOL OF BUSINESS
%   SARGENT COLLEGE OF HEALTH AND REHABILITATION SCIENCES
%   ARAM V. CHOBANIAN & EDWARD AVEDISIAN SCHOOL OF MEDICINE
%   SCHOOL OF PUBLIC HEALTH
%   SCHOOL OF THEOLOGY
%   WHEELOCK COLLEGE OF EDUCATION & HUMAN DEVELOPMENT
% BU adds a warning for one of them: for Sargent, "Do NOT use 'College of Health
% and Rehabilitation Sciences - Sargent College'".
\providecommand{\buschool@name}{SCHOOL OF CANDIDATE}
\providecommand{\bupriordegrees@list}{}
\newcommand{\buschool}[1]{\renewcommand{\buschool@name}{#1}}
% Prior degrees, one per line, in the specimen's form: "B.A., University of
% Jupiter, 2015". Separate lines with \\.
\newcommand{\bupriordegrees}[1]{\renewcommand{\bupriordegrees@list}{#1}}

% A reader line. "Place a reader's official title after the name (e.g.,
% Professor of History...). It is assumed your readers are Boston University
% faculty. If a reader is not a Boston University faculty member, add the name
% of the institution after the official title." -- so #3 may be two lines.
% "Do not put 'Dr.' before any reader's name. The doctoral degree initials after
% the name already indicate that."
\newcommand{\bureader}[3]{%
	\g@addto@macro\thesis@committee{\bu@readerline{#1}{#2}{#3}}%
}
\newcommand{\bu@readerline}[3]{%
	\noindent
	% \strut is load-bearing: a \parbox whose body is empty produces a vbox with
	% no lines, and a vbox with no lines is 0pt WIDE, not 0.22\textwidth -- so
	% the whole row would shift left for any reader declared without a label.
	\parbox[t]{0.22\textwidth}{\raggedleft\strut #1}%
	\hspace{0.02\textwidth}%
	\parbox[t]{0.70\textwidth}{%
		\rule{\linewidth}{0.4pt}\par
		\vspace{0.2\baselineskip}%
		\raggedright #2\par #3
	}\par
	\vspace{1.6\baselineskip}%
}

% --- Title page --------------------------------------------------------------
% From the samples PDF, whose annotations are prescriptive, plus the guide's
% prose. The page is double spaced like the rest of the front matter.
%
% "THE TITLE OF THE DOCTORAL DISSERTATION IN ALL CAPITAL LETTERS BOLD AND
% CENTERED"; "YOUR NAME IN ALL CAPITALS BOLD AND CENTERED"; the author's name
% "should be in order of first middle last"; the year is the "Year degree
% officially conferred, not when you defended or submitted the dissertation";
% and "A Roman numeral does not appear at the bottom center of this page,
% although the page counts as page i".
%
% The word between the school name and the title is the document type --
% "Dissertation" or "Thesis" -- which \thesis@DocNoun already carries.
%
% \graddate should hold the YEAR ALONE at BU; the specimen prints "202_".
\renewcommand{\thesistitlepage}{%
	\loadgeometry{nopagenumber}%
	\begin{titlepage}%
		\thispagestyle{empty}\doublespacing\centering
		BOSTON UNIVERSITY\par
		\buschool@name\par
		\vspace{2\baselineskip}
		\thesis@DocNoun\par
		\vspace{2\baselineskip}
		\thesis@tagtitle{Title}{\textbf{\MakeUppercase{\@title}}}\par
		\vspace{2\baselineskip}
		by\par
		\vspace{2\baselineskip}
		\textbf{\MakeUppercase{\@author}}\par
		\ifx\bupriordegrees@list\@empty\else
			{\singlespacing\bupriordegrees@list\par}%
		\fi
		\vfill
		Submitted in partial fulfillment of the\\
		requirements for the degree of\par
		\vspace{\baselineskip}
		\thesis@degree\par
		\thesis@date\par
		\vspace{0.5in}
	\end{titlepage}%
	\loadgeometry{normal}%
}

% --- Readers' approval page ---------------------------------------------------
% REQUIRED, and it is page iii: the guide's preliminary-page table lists
% "Readers' Approval page (required) -- iii (counted, does not appear on page;
% unsigned - without signatures)", and the ETD checklist asks "Are the first
% three pages (in order) Title page, Copyright page, and Reader's Approval
% page?"
%
% SIGNATURES: YES, BUT NOT IN THIS COPY -- and the two halves of that are easy
% to read as a contradiction, so both are quoted here. Signatures are collected
% separately: "For COM, ENG, GMS, GRS, QST, WED, SPH, STH, and Sargent
% dissertations, Readers' Approval pages are now created and signed via
% DocuSign"; "For all other theses and dissertations: submit a paper copy of the
% approval page with original signatures ... Photocopied, scanned, faxed, or
% other methods of reproduced signatures will not be accepted." And then:
% "Be certain to include a copy of this readers' approval page WITHOUT
% signatures in the thesis or dissertation submitted electronically."
%
% So the filed PDF's page carries rules with typed names beneath them, exactly
% as the specimen shows, and that is what this renders.
\renewcommand{\thesisapprovalpage}{%
	\clearpage\thispagestyle{empty}%
	\loadgeometry{nopagenumber}\singlespacing
	\begin{center}
		\thesis@tagtitle{H1}{Approved by}\par
	\end{center}
	\vspace{3\baselineskip}
	\begingroup
	% A document that used the class's plain \committeemember still renders: the
	% role becomes the left-hand label and the official title is simply absent.
	\renewcommand{\thesis@memberline}[2]{\bu@readerline{##2}{##1}{}}%
	\thesis@committee
	\endgroup
	\loadgeometry{normal}%
}
