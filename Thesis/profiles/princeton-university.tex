% VERIFIED and set here: geometry, spacing, the title page, the absence of an
% approval page, and the accessibility position. Nothing is left to the neutral
% fallback.
%
% ON THE SOURCE HOST. Princeton is unusual: format is not the Graduate School's
% to set. "The Princeton University Archives (Mudd Library) oversees the format
% and physical form of the dissertation or master's thesis", while "The general
% requirements for the doctoral dissertation and the master's thesis are set
% forth by the Graduate School" and "The Princeton University Archives does not
% oversee the contents, order of elements (aside from the first three pages),
% citation style". The Archives document IS the filing-format authority here,
% and the submission guide says so explicitly of a conflicting source: "Where
% there are discrepancies with the Princeton University Archives Dissertation
% Formatting Requirements document, the Princeton University Archives
% requirements should be followed."
%
% AND ITS OWN WARNING ABOUT PRECEDENT, which is worth repeating to anyone
% tempted to "improve" this file from a Princeton thesis they have seen:
% "Do not rely on the form or format of bound dissertations or theses that may
% be found in the Princeton University Library or your department, or on LaTeX
% templates shared by fellow students or staff. ... The Library's requirements
% have evolved over the years, and older practices should not be used as
% precedents."

\thesisinstitution{Princeton University}

% --- Filing figures ----------------------------------------------------------
% "The dissertation PDF should have 1-inch margins on all sides (top, bottom,
% left, and right)."
%
% A SEPARATE, WIDER RULE EXISTS FOR BOUND COPIES, and it does not apply here.
% Only the "Dual Submission" subset -- students who removed content from the PDF
% to avoid copyright infringement, approved by the Graduate School -- files a
% bound copy, which has its own margin and spacing specification in the last
% section of the Archives document. This profile encodes the PDF rule, which is
% what every candidate files.
\thesissetgeometry{left=1in, right=1in, top=1in, bottom=1in}

% "The dissertation PDF must have double spacing in the main body of the text.
% Single or 1.5 spacing may be acceptable in the front or back matter, captions,
% or footnotes."
\thesissetspacing{double}

% No \thesissetchapteropening: Princeton states one margin for every page and
% names no deeper opening.
%
% No face is set. Princeton "prefer[s]" Times New Roman or Arial rather than
% requiring either -- "If using other fonts, be sure to use embeddable Type 1 or
% TrueType fonts" -- and asks for a size "equivalent in scale to 10-point Arial
% or 12-point Times New Roman", with embedded fonts. The class default meets the
% embedding and scale requirements; script, italic and ornamental display faces,
% which Princeton does forbid, are not in play.

% --- Accessibility -----------------------------------------------------------
% OPTIONAL, IN AS MANY WORDS, and that is a finding rather than a gap. Princeton
% is the clearest "not required" of the schools profiled so far, so nothing
% checkable is declared -- no \thesisrequiretagging, no document-language
% requirement, no PDF standard. Declaring any of them would put words in the
% Archives' mouth in the direction it has explicitly declined to go.
\thesisaccessibilityrequirement{Optional}
	{Accessibility is not required. "Should you wish to make your dissertation
	 or Master's thesis meet accessibility standards, please refer to the
	 following resources from the Princeton Accessibility Office."}
\thesisaccessibilityrequirement{Alt text}
	{Also optional: "While it is not required at this time, adding alt text for
	 any illustrations makes your content accessible."}
\thesisaccessibilityrequirement{Colour}
	{The one colour rule is about fidelity, not contrast: "Illustrations that
	 require color for their analysis or interpretation should be represented in
	 full color." Font colour must be black throughout.}

% --- Title page --------------------------------------------------------------
% Reproduces the Archives' Title Page Format specimen, which is binding: "The
% first page of your dissertation should be an unpaginated title page to be
% prepared as shown in our Title Page Format document, including the elements
% shown, spacing, and capitalization."
%
% Everything but the author's name, the adviser line and the date is uppercase
% on the specimen, and nothing is bold -- so the class's neutral \bfseries title
% is dropped.
%
% THE DATE IS THE BOARD OF TRUSTEES CONFERRAL DATE, and no other date may
% appear: "the title page must have the month and year of the Board of Trustees
% meeting at which your degree is conferred -- not the date on which you
% complete your dissertation or that your defense is held. No other date should
% appear on the title page. ... The date should be formatted as 'Month YYYY',
% for example, 'January 2027'." Note there is NO COMMA; the class's \graddate
% default ("May, 20XX") is out of spec, and a document filing here must set
% \graddate{January 2027}.
%
% The specimen offers "THE DEPARTMENT OF [OR PROGRAM IN]" — a choice the
% document makes, not the profile.
\providecommand{\thesisdepartmentline}{THE DEPARTMENT OF}

\renewcommand{\thesistitlepage}{%
	\loadgeometry{nopagenumber}%
	\begin{titlepage}%
		\thispagestyle{empty}\singlespacing\centering
		\vspace*{0.5in}
		\thesis@tagtitle{Title}{\MakeUppercase{\@title}}\par
		\vspace{3\baselineskip}
		\@author\par
		\vspace{3\baselineskip}
		A \MakeUppercase{\thesis@DocNoun}\par
		\vspace{2\baselineskip}
		PRESENTED TO THE FACULTY\par
		\vspace{2\baselineskip}
		OF PRINCETON UNIVERSITY\par
		\vspace{2\baselineskip}
		IN CANDIDACY FOR THE DEGREE\par
		\vspace{2\baselineskip}
		OF \MakeUppercase{\thesis@degree}\par
		\vspace{2\baselineskip}
		RECOMMENDED FOR ACCEPTANCE BY\par
		\vspace{2\baselineskip}
		\thesisdepartmentline\par
		\vspace{2\baselineskip}
		\MakeUppercase{\thesis@program}\par
		\vspace{3\baselineskip}
		Adviser: \thesis@advisor\par
		\vspace{2\baselineskip}
		\thesis@date\par
	\end{titlepage}%
	\loadgeometry{normal}%
}

% --- Committee approval page --------------------------------------------------
% There is none in the manuscript, and the title page above carries the approval
% statement instead: "RECOMMENDED FOR ACCEPTANCE BY / THE DEPARTMENT OF ...",
% over the adviser's name.
%
% THIS IS AN INFERENCE FROM THREE FACTS RATHER THAN A SINGLE PROHIBITING QUOTE,
% so a reviewer should look at it:
%   1. The opening pages are prescribed and closed: "The first three pages of
%      your dissertation, in order, should be the Title page, the Copyright (or
%      Creative Commons license) page, and the Abstract. If your submission does
%      not meet these requirements, you will be contacted via email from the
%      Mudd Library to make changes." An approval leaf cannot go where every
%      other school puts it.
%   2. The Archives' complete 8-page formatting specification never mentions a
%      signature, approval or committee page anywhere.
%   3. Approval is collected OUTSIDE the manuscript: each principal reader
%      submits a signed dissertation reader report to the department, and the
%      Graduate School's PhD Dissertation Report Form carries the signatures.
%
% Left unset, the class's neutral committee page with signature rules would
% print -- a page Princeton has no place for. Emitting nothing is the safer of
% the two errors here, and the reasoning above is the whole of the case.
\thesisnoapprovalpage
