%          Dissertation", and "Instructions for Preparation of the Thesis or
%          Dissertation" — the Graduate School's own filing requirements)
% source: https://gsbs.uth.edu/academics/assets/forms/Template-for-the-Thesis-or-Dissertation-03212023.docx
%         (the Graduate School's official Word template, linked from the page
%          above; its filename carries its revision date, 2023-03-21)
% accessed: 2026-09-05
% checked:  texlib-thesis-profile-round
%
% VERIFIED and set here: geometry, spacing, the title page, and the approval
% page. NOT set: chapter-opening margin (GSBS states no deeper opening margin),
% and no accessibility check-flag is raised — see the accessibility note below.
%
% WHICH GRADUATE SCHOOL THIS IS. IPEDS records the degree-granting institution
% as "The University of Texas MD Anderson Cancer Center", but the graduate
% school that sets and enforces these rules is the joint MD Anderson Cancer
% Center UTHealth Houston Graduate School of Biomedical Sciences (GSBS), which
% is where the requirements live. \thesisinstitution below is the IPEDS name,
% per RESEARCHING.md; the pages themselves print the GSBS name, because that is
% the name the template puts on them.
%
% ORDERING NOTE FOR ANYONE FILING HERE. GSBS puts the Approval Page FIRST, ahead
% of the Title Page: "Arrangement of the Thesis or Dissertation: Approval Page,
% Title Page, Copyright*, Dedication*, ...". Most templates in this library call
% \thesistitlepage before \thesisapprovalpage. Call them the other way round for
% a GSBS filing. The profile cannot enforce the order — that is the document's.

\thesisinstitution{The University of Texas MD Anderson Cancer Center}

% --- Filing figures ----------------------------------------------------------
% "MARGINS- Left margin must be 1.25 inches or greater. Top, bottom, and right
% margins must be 0.8 inches or greater."
%
% Note this is stated as MINIMA, not as exact figures, which is unusual. The
% left minimum is binding and is set exactly. For the other three the rule
% permits anything from 0.8in up, so this profile follows the school's own
% template, whose section properties set top/right/bottom to 1440 twips = 1in
% (and left to 1800 twips = 1.25in, agreeing with the prose). 1in is therefore
% both in spec and the figure GSBS itself ships; 0.8in would also be in spec.
\thesissetgeometry{left=1.25in, right=1in, top=1in, bottom=1in}

% "TEXT- The text must be double-spaced." Stated of the text; the bibliography
% is called out separately as double-spaced too ("References must be
% double-spaced just like the rest of the thesis"). The one exception GSBS
% names is quoted matter from others' publications, which is "indented 1/2 inch
% from the left margin, single-spaced" — a per-quotation rule, not a document
% one, so it is not expressible here and is noted rather than encoded.
\thesissetspacing{double}

% No chapter-opening rule: GSBS states one margin rule and no deeper top margin
% for chapter or major-section openings, so \thesissetchapteropening is left
% unset and openings run at the body's 1in.

% --- Accessibility -----------------------------------------------------------
% NOTHING IS DECLARED REQUIRED HERE, deliberately. The full text of the
% graduation page (the filing requirements) contains no occurrence of
% "accessible", "accessibility", "alt text", "WCAG", "tagged" or "PDF/A"; the
% forms page carries none either; and a site search of gsbs.uth.edu for
% "accessibility" returns only disability-accommodation and event-access
% contacts, not a document requirement. So no \thesisrequiretagging,
% \thesisrequiredocumentlanguage or \thesisrequirepdfstandard is raised —
% declaring one would put a rule in GSBS's mouth that GSBS has not published.
\thesisaccessibilityrequirement{Not published}
	{No accessibility requirement for a filed thesis or dissertation is
	 published in the MD Anderson UTHealth Houston GSBS filing requirements as
	 read on 2026-09-05. This is a finding, not a licence: an untagged PDF is
	 still an inaccessible one, and other UT-system graduate schools have begun
	 adding such rules. Re-check before filing.}

% --- Title page --------------------------------------------------------------
% Reproduced from the official template, whose title-page paragraphs are all
% centred. The line breaks below are the template's own; the bracketed
% alternatives in the template ("Thesis [or Dissertation]", "Master of Science
% [or Doctor of Philosophy]") are resolved from the document's \doctype.
%
% Two template details worth keeping: the article "A" sits alone on its own
% line above the document noun, and the author line asks for "Student's Full
% Name (given name first) and Previous Degrees" — so \author should carry the
% prior degrees, e.g. \author{Ada N. Student, B.S.}.
%
% GSBS forbids a page number here ("No page number should appear on the Approval
% Page or the Title Page, although both should be included in the count"), which
% is what \loadgeometry{nopagenumber} plus \thispagestyle{empty} give.
\renewcommand{\thesistitlepage}{%
	\loadgeometry{nopagenumber}%
	\begin{titlepage}%
		\thispagestyle{empty}\singlespacing\centering
		\vspace*{\fill}
		\thesis@tagtitle{Title}{{\large\bfseries\@title}}\par
		\vfill
		A\\
		\thesis@DocNoun\par
		\vfill
		Presented to the Faculty of\\
		The University of Texas\\
		MD Anderson Cancer Center UTHealth Houston\\
		Graduate School of Biomedical Sciences\par
		\vfill
		in Partial Fulfillment\\
		of the Requirements\\
		for the Degree of\\
		\thesis@degree\par
		\vfill
		by\par
		\vspace{\baselineskip}
		\@author\par
		\vspace{\baselineskip}
		Houston, Texas\par
		\thesis@date\par
		\vspace*{\fill}
	\end{titlepage}%
	\loadgeometry{normal}%
}

% --- Committee approval page --------------------------------------------------
% Also reproduced from the official template. Its shape: the title, "by", and
% the author centred at the top; then a LEFT-ALIGNED block headed "APPROVED:"
% carrying one signature rule per committee member with the name typed beneath;
% then a second "APPROVED:" with a rule for the Dean.
%
% PHYSICAL SIGNATURES ARE STILL COLLECTED. "Submit the original thesis approval
% page signed by your committee, the results of your defense forms ... to Bunny
% Perez in Academic Affairs", and before that "a separate copy of just the
% approval page with your advisory committee members' signatures. Please note
% that the Dean's signature will be added to your approval page later". So the
% rules below are real signature lines, not decoration, and the Dean's rule is
% left blank on purpose — the Dean signs it after the student submits.
%
% The role, where a document gives one, is typed UNDER the name rather than
% after it: the template shows "Supervisor Name, Degree(s)" on one line and
% "Advisory Professor" on the next, and gives the other members no role line at
% all. \thesis@memberline is redefined for this page to match, so a document
% that writes \committeemember{Pat D. Advisor, Ph.D.}{Advisory Professor} and
% \committeemember{Sam K. Reader, Ph.D.}{} lands exactly on the template.
\renewcommand{\thesisapprovalpage}{%
	\clearpage\thispagestyle{empty}%
	\loadgeometry{nopagenumber}\singlespacing
	\thesis@tagtitle{H1}{{\color{white}\tiny Approval Page}}\par
	\begin{center}
		{\bfseries\@title}\par
		\vspace{\baselineskip}
		by\par
		\vspace{\baselineskip}
		\@author\par
	\end{center}
	\vspace{2\baselineskip}
	\begingroup
	\raggedright
	\renewcommand{\thesis@memberline}[2]{%
		\vskip 3em
		\rule{2.75in}{0.4pt}\par
		##1\par
		\ifx\relax##2\relax\else ##2\par\fi
	}%
	APPROVED:\par
	\thesis@committee
	\vspace{3\baselineskip}
	APPROVED:\par
	\vskip 3em
	\rule{2.75in}{0.4pt}\par
	Dean, The University of Texas\par
	MD Anderson Cancer Center UTHealth Houston\par
	Graduate School of Biomedical Sciences\par
	\endgroup
	\loadgeometry{normal}\doublespacing\clearpage
}
