% A DISTINCTION THAT IS EASY TO GET BACKWARDS. USC leaves the STYLE MANUAL to
% the discipline — "Please consult with your staff graduate advisor, or
% committee chair, regarding an acceptable manuscript style, such as MLA, APA,
% or Chicago" — while prescribing the GENERAL FORMATTING itself. Third-party
% summaries collapse the two and report that USC "does not require any specific
% set of formatting guidelines". That is true of the citation style and false of
% the margins, which are stated flatly below.

\thesisinstitution{University of Southern California}

% --- Filing figures ----------------------------------------------------------
% "All margins must be 1""  -- stated once, for all four sides, with no range,
% no minimum/maximum and no exception.
%
% The companion rule is a footer rule, not a margin: "Footer margin for page
% numbers must be 0.5" from bottom of page", and "With the exception of page
% numbers, all other manuscript material must fit within the margin
% requirements." The class's geometry carries `includefoot', so the page number
% lives inside the 1in text block rather than 0.5in from the paper edge. That is
% CONSERVATIVE, not out of spec -- USC's rule places the number at 0.5in as an
% allowance below the text, and material sitting further inside the margin
% breaks nothing. It is recorded here because it is the one figure on this page
% the class does not reproduce exactly, and a reviewer may prefer to chase it.
\thesissetgeometry{left=1in, right=1in, top=1in, bottom=1in}

% "Double space text throughout manuscript and indent paragraphs". A requirement,
% not a menu. The single-spaced exceptions USC names -- footnotes, long quoted
% passages, figure and table captions, items in lists and tables -- are
% per-element and not expressible in a profile.
\thesissetspacing{double}

% No chapter-opening rule is published, so \thesissetchapteropening stays unset.

% --- Accessibility -----------------------------------------------------------
% NOTHING IS DECLARED REQUIRED. The 13-page guidelines PDF contains ZERO
% occurrences of "accessib", "alt text", "WCAG", "tagged", "PDF/A" or "screen
% read"; the Manuscript Formatting Guidelines page has none; and neither does
% "Guidelines to Submission" or "Required Documents". USC is a private
% institution, so the April 2026 ADA Title II date that drives several public
% universities' rules does not reach it in the same way.
\thesisaccessibilityrequirement{Not published}
	{USC's Graduate School publishes no accessibility requirement for a filed
	 thesis or dissertation, in the formatting guidelines or on any of the three
	 submission pages, as read on 2026-09-05. A finding, not a licence -- and
	 one to re-check, since the governing PDF dates from 2020.}

% --- Copyright year (used by the title page below) -----------------------------
% USC's title page carries a copyright line, and the class has no year macro.
% Set it in the document preamble:  \thesiscopyrightyear{2027}
% If it is unset the line is omitted and the title page says so at build time,
% because USC's instruction is to "format exactly as shown" and every specimen
% in Appendix A carries the line.
\newcommand{\thesis@copyrightyear}{}
\newcommand{\thesiscopyrightyear}[1]{\renewcommand{\thesis@copyrightyear}{#1}}

% --- Title page --------------------------------------------------------------
% "See Appendix A for title page templates and format exactly as shown", "Text
% surrounded by [...] on the templates indicate information unique to each
% student and should be revised accordingly", and -- note this one --
% "Do not bold text or add any extraneous information."
%
% THE TITLE IS THEREFORE NOT BOLD, unlike the class's neutral title page and
% unlike most of the other profiles in this directory. Do not "fix" it.
%
% Reproduced from the PhD specimen (Appendix A, p. 8 of the PDF), rendered at 90
% dpi and read as an image. Its shape: title and subtitle at the top; "by" and
% the author below them; then a single tight seven-line block; then the
% conferral date; then, at the very foot, a two-column line with "Copyright
% [Year]" flush left and the author flush right.
%
% The seven-line block is quoted exactly and must not be reflowed:
%
%   A Dissertation Presented to the
%   FACULTY OF THE USC GRADUATE SCHOOL
%   UNIVERSITY OF SOUTHERN CALIFORNIA
%   In Partial Fulfillment of the
%   Requirements for the Degree
%   DOCTOR OF PHILOSOPHY
%   [(MAJOR or DEGREE PROGRAM NAME)]
%
% A NOTE ON THE SPECIMEN SET, because a filer needs it. Appendix A holds SIX
% specimens, and they do not all say the same thing. The PhD one names "FACULTY
% OF THE USC GRADUATE SCHOOL" and spells the degree "DOCTOR OF PHILOSOPHY"; the
% other doctorates name "FACULTY OF THE USC [SCHOOL NAME]" and read "DOCTOR OF
% [MAJOR OR DEGREE PROGRAM NAME]"; the master's ones read "A Thesis Presented to
% the ... FACULTY OF THE USC [SCHOOL NAME] ... MASTER OF ARTS". This profile
% follows the PhD/Graduate School specimen, because that is the one addressed to
% the Graduate School itself. A filer in a named school should replace the
% FACULTY line accordingly; the profile cannot know which school.
%
% The date: "Degrees are conferred three times per year: December (for fall),
% May (for spring), August (for summer)" and "Only the month and year are
% required; the day is not." So \graddate{May 2027} is the right shape here.
%
% "Every page in the manuscript, except the title page, must have a page number"
% -- hence the no-footer geometry and \thispagestyle{empty}.
\renewcommand{\thesistitlepage}{%
	\loadgeometry{nopagenumber}%
	\begin{titlepage}%
		\thispagestyle{empty}\singlespacing\centering
		\vspace*{1.5in}
		\thesis@tagtitle{Title}{\@title}\par
		\vspace{3\baselineskip}
		by\par
		\vspace{2\baselineskip}
		\@author\par
		\vspace{5\baselineskip}
		A \thesis@DocNoun{} Presented to the\\
		FACULTY OF THE USC GRADUATE SCHOOL\\
		UNIVERSITY OF SOUTHERN CALIFORNIA\\
		In Partial Fulfillment of the\\
		Requirements for the Degree\\
		\MakeUppercase{\thesis@degree}\\
		(\MakeUppercase{\thesis@program})\par
		\vspace{4\baselineskip}
		\thesis@date\par
		\vfill
		% The specimen's foot: Copyright + year flush left, author flush right.
		\ifx\thesis@copyrightyear\@empty
			\ClassWarningNoLine{texlib-thesis}{USC's title-page specimens all carry a^^J%
				copyright line, and \string\thesiscopyrightyear{} is unset, so it was^^J%
				omitted. Put \string\thesiscopyrightyear{<year>} in the preamble}%
		\else
			\noindent\makebox[\textwidth][s]{%
				\parbox[t]{0.45\textwidth}{\raggedright Copyright \thesis@copyrightyear}%
				\hfill
				\parbox[t]{0.45\textwidth}{\raggedleft \@author}%
			}\par
		\fi
	\end{titlepage}%
	\loadgeometry{normal}%
}

% --- Committee approval page --------------------------------------------------
% DELIBERATELY LEFT AS THE CLASS'S NEUTRAL FALLBACK, and this is the one
% judgement in the file worth arguing with.
%
% USC's rule, quoted in full:
%
%   "A signature page is not required by the University for thesis or
%    dissertation submission. Students who are required by their school,
%    department or program to include a signature page should consult their
%    committee chairperson and include the signature page in the uploaded PDF
%    version of the thesis/dissertation manuscript."
%
% That is neither of the two cases the class has a hook for. It is not a
% prescribed page, so \renewcommand{\thesisapprovalpage}{...} would be inventing
% a layout USC does not publish. But it is also NOT a prohibition, so
% \thesisnoapprovalpage would be wrong in the opposite direction: it makes
% \thesisapprovalpage print nothing, which would silently drop the page for
% exactly the student whose department requires one -- the person USC's sentence
% is addressed to.
%
% So the neutral page renders. It is not USC's page, because USC has none; it is
% a plausible signature page for a student whose department asks for one, and
% that student's chairperson is the authority on its wording, which is what USC
% says. \thesisapprovalpage is simply not called by a student whose department
% does not require it.
%
% PHYSICAL SIGNATURES: not by the University. Where a department does require
% the page, USC asks for it inside the uploaded PDF rather than as a separate
% administrative file, which is the opposite of Vanderbilt's arrangement.
