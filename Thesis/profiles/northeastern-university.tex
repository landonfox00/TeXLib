% PARTIAL, AND DELIBERATELY SO. Northeastern has no university-wide filing
% format: the rules are the COLLEGE's, and the colleges do not agree about the
% one figure a profile most wants. Encoding any college's margin as
% "Northeastern's" would be wrong for every candidate in another college, which
% is the failure mode RESEARCHING.md exists to prevent. So the geometry and
% spacing are left to the class's neutral defaults and the college figures are
% recorded below instead, ready to be pasted into a document preamble.
%
% VERIFIED and set here: the institution name, and the absence of an approval
% page in the filed document.
% NOT SET, because it is college-specific: geometry, spacing, the chapter
% opening, and the title page.
%
% ---------------------------------------------------------------------------
% WHAT EACH COLLEGE PUBLISHES (read on the access date)
%
% College of Engineering — the only one of the three with complete figures:
%     "Page Margins  Left: 1.5"; Right: 1"; Top: 1.0"; Bottom: 1.0". The margin
%      requirements are standard for a bound thesis."
%     "Spacing of all text within the thesis, including references, should be
%      either 1.5 or double-spaced."
%     Type size "neither less than 10 point font nor more than 12 point font".
%     Major headings sit "two inches from the top of the page, with the text
%      beginning three single-space lines below" — i.e. a candidate in COE wants
%      \thesissetchapteropening{2in} as well.
%     Page numbers "in the upper right corner, ½ inch below the top edge".
%     "Title pages are technically the first numbered pages (Roman numeral 'i').
%      However, the numeral 'i' does not appear on the title page."
%   A COE document therefore wants, in its own preamble:
%     \thesissetgeometry{left=1.5in, right=1in, top=1in, bottom=1in}
%     \thesissetspacing{onehalf}   % or double
%     \thesissetchapteropening{2in}
%   Note the style guide's own cover reads "Revised 2016" and is the older of
%   the two COE documents; the submission procedures are "Revised: 2021".
%
% College of Social Sciences and Humanities — publishes NO margin, spacing or
%   type figure of its own. Its formatting page points candidates at ProQuest's
%   "Preparing Your Manuscript" guide and at a format-review appointment with
%   the Graduate Office. ProQuest's guide states ProQuest's requirements, not
%   Northeastern's, so nothing from it is reproduced here.
%
% College of Science — the 2017–18 preparation guide states no margin rule
%   either; it is procedural.
% ---------------------------------------------------------------------------
%
% TO FINISH THIS PROFILE someone has to decide what an institution-level profile
% should mean for a university that files by college. The two honest options are
% to leave this as the record of the split, or to add per-college profiles
% (northeastern-university-coe, ...) — which is a change to how profiles are
% named, and therefore a person's decision, not this routine's.

\thesisinstitution{Northeastern University}

% --- Accessibility -----------------------------------------------------------
% Searched all five documents above for "accessib", "alt text", "tagged",
% "WCAG" and "PDF/A": no match in any of them. The only document-format demand
% any of them makes is COE's "(PDF) with embedded fonts. This is the only format
% you may upload to the ETD Administrator."
\thesisaccessibilityrequirement{Not published}
	{Neither Northeastern nor any of the three colleges whose guides were read
	 publishes an accessibility requirement for a filed thesis or dissertation.
	 The only format demand found is the College of Engineering's, that the ETD
	 be "(PDF) with embedded fonts".}

% --- Title page --------------------------------------------------------------
% College-specific, and only COE prints a specimen. The class's neutral title
% page renders instead. A COE candidate should follow that college's example
% page, which requires the full official title, the author's full name as
% registered, the degree ("Master of Science" for all master's students) and the
% graduation date, centred between the margins.

% --- Committee approval page --------------------------------------------------
% NOT in the filed document, at any of the three colleges. Signatures are
% collected on a separate form — the NU Graduate Approval Record — and the
% manuscript uploaded to ProQuest does not carry them:
%
%   COE, submission procedures, step (f): "Do not include signature page in the
%   final ETD submission."
%   CSSH, upload checklist: "Bring the prepared Approval Record to the defense.
%   Collect committee member signatures... Send the completed Approval Record to
%   gradcssh@northeastern.edu."
%   COS: "Finalize your edits and obtain signatures on the NU Graduate Approval
%   Record", submitted with the Department Chair's signature.
%
% COE is the only one that forbids the page in so many words; the other two
% route signatures to the same separate record. No Northeastern source read
% requires an approval page inside the manuscript.
\thesisnoapprovalpage
