% 1. THE FORMATTING GUIDE IS OLD, AND IT IS STILL THE CURRENT ONE. Every page is
%    footed "Revised 9/2010" and its sample title page is dated May 2005, but
%    the live Forms page links exactly this PDF and OHSU publishes no other
%    formatting document. Its modal is unusually soft -- "We often receive
%    questions about thesis and dissertation formatting guidelines. We recommend
%    that you follow disciplinary conventions whenever possible. But for most
%    cases, this formatting guide MAY BE HELPFUL." The figures below are set
%    because they are the only figures OHSU publishes, not because OHSU commands
%    them; a filer should confirm with their program, which the guide itself
%    says may impose more: "The student should consult the advisor/mentor or the
%    Program Director for information about any additional requirements."
%
% 2. THE APPROVAL PAGE HAS MOVED OUT OF THE DOCUMENT SINCE 2010. The old guide
%    says "The second page of the document should be the CERTIFICATE OF APPROVAL
%    page", and prints a sample with signature lines. The live Forms page now
%    lists "Dissertation/Thesis Certificate of Approval" as a SMARTSHEET FORM
%    (app.smartsheet.com/b/form/...) and says of it: "This replaces the
%    'signature page' of your thesis, with which your Oral Exam Committee
%    approves of the final revisions of your dissertation. The approved form is
%    due before the last day of the term of intended graduation." A Smartsheet
%    submission cannot be bound into a PDF, and "replaces" is the operative
%    word. \thesisnoapprovalpage below follows the live page, not the 2010
%    sample -- flagged here because the sample is still printed in the guide and
%    a later reader will find it.

% The ampersand is part of the IPEDS name and must be escaped — a bare & here is
% a "Misplaced alignment tab character" the moment the class typesets the name.
\thesisinstitution{Oregon Health \& Science University}

% --- Filing figures ----------------------------------------------------------
% "The left-hand margin should be at least 1.5 inches wide, the top and
% right-hand margins 1.0 inch and the bottom margin not less than 1.0 inch."
%
% Note the asymmetry in how these are stated: left and bottom are MINIMA ("at
% least", "not less than"), top and right are exact. The minima are taken as the
% setting, which is in spec either way. The left margin is unconditional -- it is
% a binding allowance stated without a binding condition -- so `twoside' must
% not alternate it.
\thesissetgeometry{left=1.5in, right=1in, top=1in, bottom=1in}

% "All typing or printing should be double-spaced except footnotes. Footnotes
% should be single-spaced with a double space between footnotes." Tables are the
% one other exception: "Tables may be single or double-spaced but must conform to
% the margin requirements".
\thesissetspacing{double}

% No \thesissetchapteropening. OHSU does prescribe a deeper opening for ONE page
% only -- "The heading of the Table of Contents should be typed in capital
% letters not less than 2 inches from the top of the page" -- and this hook
% applies to every chapter and major-section opening, which would overshoot. A
% document can get the ToC right locally; encoding it here would silently move
% every chapter opening on the strength of a rule about one page.

% --- Accessibility -----------------------------------------------------------
% NOT PUBLISHED by Graduate Studies. Searched the whole formatting guide for
% accessib / alt text / WCAG / tagged / screen reader / PDF/A / Title II: zero
% occurrences -- unsurprising for a 2010 document -- and the live Forms,
% Policies and Resources page carries none either.
%
% ONE STONE LEFT UNTURNED, recorded so the next pass does not repeat the
% attempt: OHSU deposits go to the Library at digitalcollections.ohsu.edu, whose
% submission-information page answered every fetch with a bot-protection
% interstitial (HTTP 202, empty body) on the access date. If OHSU publishes a
% deposit accessibility rule anywhere, that page is where it would be -- and it
% would be the Library's rule rather than Graduate Studies'.
\thesisaccessibilityrequirement{Not published}
	{OHSU School of Medicine Graduate Studies publishes no accessibility
	 requirement for a filed thesis or dissertation. Its formatting guide
	 predates the question entirely (revised 2010), and its current Forms,
	 Policies and Resources page states none.}

% --- Title page --------------------------------------------------------------
% Reproduces the guide's "[Sample Title Page]". Its bracketed annotations are
% instructions, not content: "A THESIS/DISSERTATION [Select appropriate label]"
% and "Doctor of Philosophy/Master of Science/Master of Public Health [Select
% only one]" -- so the class's \thesis@DocNoun and \thesis@degree make both
% selections.
%
% The department line names the DEPARTMENT and then the institution in three
% lines: "Presented to the Department of Physiology & Pharmacology / and the
% Oregon Health & Science University / School of Medicine". \thesis@program
% supplies the department name; the profile supplies the rest, and the school
% line is exposed for the units of OHSU that are not the School of Medicine --
% the guide is a School of Medicine document and says so in its own header.
%
% NOTE FOR THE DOCUMENT: the specimen's line reads "Presented to the Department
% of Physiology & Pharmacology", so \gradprogram must carry the WHOLE phrase
% ("Department of Mathematics"), not the bare subject — the profile supplies
% only the word "the" before it. Any ampersand in that name needs escaping, as
% the institution name above did.
%
% The specimen sets the title in capitals and the rest in ordinary case, with
% the date as month and year.
\providecommand{\thesisschoolline}{School of Medicine}

\renewcommand{\thesistitlepage}{%
	\loadgeometry{nopagenumber}%
	\begin{titlepage}%
		\thispagestyle{empty}\singlespacing\centering
		\vspace*{\fill}
		\thesis@tagtitle{Title}{\MakeUppercase{\@title}}\par
		\vspace{3\baselineskip}
		By\\
		\@author\par
		\vspace{3\baselineskip}
		A \MakeUppercase{\thesis@DocNoun}\par
		\vspace{3\baselineskip}
		Presented to the \thesis@program\\
		and the Oregon Health \& Science University\\
		\thesisschoolline\\
		in partial fulfillment of\\
		the requirements for the degree of\par
		\vspace{2\baselineskip}
		\thesis@degree\par
		\vspace{2\baselineskip}
		\thesis@date\par
		\vspace*{\fill}
	\end{titlepage}%
	\loadgeometry{normal}%
}

% --- Committee approval page --------------------------------------------------
% None in the filed document. See point 2 of the header: the Certificate of
% Approval is now a Smartsheet form that "replaces the 'signature page' of your
% thesis". Signatures are still collected -- the Oral Exam Committee approves
% the final revisions through that form, due before the last day of the term of
% intended graduation -- but not on a page inside the manuscript.
%
% A filer whose program still wants the 2010 bound Certificate of Approval page
% must build it themselves; this profile deliberately does not emit one, because
% the class's neutral page would otherwise print a committee page OHSU's current
% process has replaced.
\thesisnoapprovalpage
