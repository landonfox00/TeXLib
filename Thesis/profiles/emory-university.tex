\thesisinstitution{Emory University}

% --- Filing figures ----------------------------------------------------------
% "Margins: All materials (including text, illustrations, graphs, figures, etc.)
% must be within the margins (all margins = 1"). A dissertation/thesis cannot be
% accepted if any of the text falls outside the margins."
%
% Note the second sentence: Emory says outright that a margin violation is
% grounds for rejection, and the rule is reasserted for the special pages --
% "Remember that the page margin rules apply to all pages, including the special
% pages, like the Approval Sheet."
\thesissetgeometry{left=1in, right=1in, top=1in, bottom=1in}

% "Spacing and Font: Double spacing is required." A requirement, flatly.
% The exceptions Emory names are per-page, not per-element, and are listed here
% rather than encoded: "Two of the special pages may be single-spaced: the
% Distribution Agreement and the Abstract", and the font-size rule excludes
% "foot notes, abstracts, and acknowledgements".
\thesissetspacing{double}

% No chapter-opening rule is published, so \thesissetchapteropening stays unset.

% --- Accessibility -----------------------------------------------------------
% NOTHING IS DECLARED REQUIRED. Across the 2023 guidelines, the 2019 PDF and the
% ETD repository page, the string "accessib" occurs exactly twice and neither is
% a document rule: once in the Distribution Agreement's licence grant ("the
% non-exclusive license to archive, make accessible, and display my thesis or
% dissertation"), and once about publishers regarding "an electronically
% accessible dissertation as a prior publication". There is no occurrence of
% "alt text", "WCAG", "tagged" or "PDF/A" anywhere. Emory is private, so the
% April 2026 ADA Title II date does not reach it as it does the public
% universities in this directory.
\thesisaccessibilityrequirement{Not published}
	{Emory's Laney Graduate School publishes no accessibility requirement for a
	 filed thesis or dissertation, in either edition of the guidelines or on the
	 ETD page, as read on 2026-09-05. The word "accessible" appears only in the
	 Distribution Agreement's licence grant and in a note about prior
	 publication. A finding, not a licence.}

% --- Cover page (Emory's name for the title page) -------------------------------
% "The cover page should be designed according to the plan below." Reproduced
% from that plan (2019 PDF p. 15, rendered at 85 dpi and read as an image; the
% 2023 edition carries the same block).
%
% Everything is centred and plain -- no uppercasing, no bold. The line breaks in
% the closing statement are the specimen's own and must not be reflowed:
%
%   A [thesis/dissertation] submitted to the Faculty of the
%   James T. Laney School of Graduate Studies of Emory University
%   in partial fulfillment of the requirements for the degree of
%   [Doctor of Philosophy/Master of Arts/Science/etc.]
%   in [field]
%   [year]
%
% Details from Emory's own comments on that page:
%
%   * "Previous academic degree: List your degree, the name of the university,
%     and the year you received the degree. For example, 'B.A., Yale University,
%     2004'". It sits on its OWN LINE under the author's name, so a document
%     filing here should write \author{Ada N. Student\\B.A., Yale University,
%     2004} -- the class has one author field and Emory wants two lines.
%   * "Your degree does not include a subject matter: your degree is not 'Doctor
%     of Philosophy, Sociology' or any such combination." So \graddegree{Doctor
%     of Philosophy} alone.
%   * "Field: Generally, your field is the subject-matter name of your program
%     ... The field does not include the word 'program', and does not include
%     any sub-field you specialize in." That is \gradprogram.
%   * "Year: Write the calendar year you receive your degree (not the academic
%     year)." The cover page shows the YEAR ALONE, so \graddate{2027}.
%   * "Advisor: If your committee had co-chairs, list both, each noted as
%     'Advisor.'" and "Ask your advisor/s how he or she or they should be
%     listed."
%
% The cover page is one of the "special pages", which "should not be numbered,
% but must otherwise follow the formatting instructions" -- hence the no-footer
% geometry and \thispagestyle{empty}, with the 1in margins unchanged.
\renewcommand{\thesistitlepage}{%
	\loadgeometry{nopagenumber}%
	\begin{titlepage}%
		\thispagestyle{empty}\singlespacing\centering
		\vspace*{1in}
		\thesis@tagtitle{Title}{\@title}\par
		\vspace{3\baselineskip}
		By\par
		\vspace{3\baselineskip}
		\@author\par
		\vspace{3\baselineskip}
		Advisor: \thesis@advisor\par
		\vspace{6\baselineskip}
		A \thesis@docnoun{} submitted to the Faculty of the\\
		James T. Laney School of Graduate Studies of Emory University\\
		in partial fulfillment of the requirements for the degree of\\
		\thesis@degree\\
		in \thesis@program\\
		\thesis@date\par
		\vspace*{\fill}
	\end{titlepage}%
	\loadgeometry{normal}%
}

% --- Approval page --------------------------------------------------------------
% Emory calls it the APPROVAL PAGE (the specimen heading says "Approval Sheet")
% and it IS part of the filed manuscript: "A signed Approval Page -- signed by
% all members of your committee" is a checklist item, and "If you make this page
% for the ETD manuscript, include an unsigned version."
%
% THE RULES ARE BLANK ON PURPOSE. "Do NOT include real signatures in the
% electronic copy of your full dissertation/thesis that you upload into the ETD"
% and "No real signatures should be included in the ETD submission". So this
% page draws empty signature rules, which is exactly right for the ETD copy --
% it is not an oversight, and nothing should be filled in.
%
% PHYSICAL SIGNATURES ARE STILL COLLECTED, SEPARATELY, AND ELECTRONICALLY. The
% signed version comes from a DocuSign Power Form ("These two pages are a single
% combined DocuSign Power Form -- you will receive a pdf of all signatures once
% completed") and goes to the Laney Graduate School with the completion packet,
% not into the ETD upload. Emory also permits the signed DocuSign pages to be
% placed in front of the manuscript, at the student's option.
%
% Structure, from the specimen: title, "By", author + degree, field; then one
% centred signature rule per member with the typed name and role beneath;
% then "Accepted:", the Dean's rule, name and title, and a separate shorter rule
% over the word "Date". The Dean's rule stays blank too -- "You Do Not need to
% get the Dean's signature on this form before you submit your graduation forms."
%
% Ordering rules Emory states and this class cannot enforce: "Advisor: If your
% committee had co-chairs, refer to each of them as Co-Advisor, and list them in
% alphabetical order" and "Committee Members: List the members in alphabetical
% order." A document must supply \committeemember in that order.
%
% NOT REPRODUCED, and flagged rather than guessed: "with committees of 5 or 6
% members, you may use a two-column layout for the committee members." That is
% permissive, and a committee of four fits the single column, so the single
% column is used. A filer with six members whose page overruns the 1in bottom
% margin must build the two-column variant by hand -- and Emory is explicit that
% overrunning is grounds for rejection.
\renewcommand{\thesisapprovalpage}{%
	\clearpage\thispagestyle{empty}%
	\loadgeometry{nopagenumber}\singlespacing
	\thesis@tagtitle{H1}{{\color{white}\tiny Approval Page}}\par
	% VERTICAL SPACING IS DELIBERATELY TIGHT. Emory says a page whose material
	% falls outside the margins "cannot be accepted", and this page grows by four
	% lines per committee member. A first draft with roomier gaps pushed the Date
	% rule off the bottom of the page with a committee of only three. The gaps
	% below are sized so a committee of FIVE still fits inside the 1in bottom
	% margin -- which is the size Emory says the single-column layout should
	% "likely accommodate". At six, use Emory's permitted two-column layout.
	\begin{center}
		\@title\par
		\vspace{\baselineskip}
		By\par
		\vspace{\baselineskip}
		\@author\par
		\vspace{\baselineskip}
		\thesis@program\par
		% One blank rule per member, typed name and role beneath it.
		\begingroup
		\renewcommand{\thesis@memberline}[2]{%
			\vskip 1.4em
			\rule{4in}{0.4pt}\par
			##1\par
			\ifx\relax##2\relax\else ##2\par\fi
		}%
		\thesis@committee
		\endgroup
		\vspace{\baselineskip}
		Accepted:\par
		\vskip 1.4em
		\rule{4in}{0.4pt}\par
		% The Dean's name is from the 2023-10-03 edition and is the one line in
		% this file that is certain to go stale -- it changed once already.
		Kimberly Jacob Arriola, Ph.D, MPH\par
		Dean of the James T. Laney School of Graduate Studies\par
		\vskip 1.4em
		\rule{2in}{0.4pt}\par
		Date\par
	\end{center}%
	\loadgeometry{normal}\doublespacing\clearpage
}
