% --- Accessibility -----------------------------------------------------------
% Ohio State has had required digital-accessibility features since Spring 2023
% and publishes a Graduate School ETD Accessibility Plan. It is also blunt about
% who does the work: "The Graduate School cannot provide hands on training or
% editing of a document to meet formatting and digital accessibility standards."
\thesisrequiretagging
\thesisrequiredocumentlanguage
\thesisaccessibilityrequirement{Required since Spring 2023}
	{Digital accessibility features are a stated requirement of a filed
	 document, governed by the Graduate School's Electronic Thesis and
	 Dissertation Accessibility Plan.}
\thesisaccessibilityrequirement{Permission flag}
	{"PDF accessibility permission flag is checked" -- the PDF must not be
	 secured against the text extraction a screen reader depends on. A tagged
	 build from this class sets no security handler, so this holds unless
	 something downstream re-encrypts the file.}
\thesisaccessibilityrequirement{Checked by you}
	{Students are directed to run a digital accessibility checker themselves
	 (Adobe Acrobat Pro, available through the university), not to rely on the
	 Graduate School's format review to catch it.}
