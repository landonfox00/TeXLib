\thesisinstitution{Dartmouth College}

% "For binding purposes, the left-hand margin must be 1.5 inches wide on all
% pages of the thesis including figures, appendices, etc. The top, bottom, and
% right-hand margins should be between 0.5" to 1" wide."
%
% The left figure is exact and is a binding allowance. The other three are a
% RANGE, and this profile takes the wide end of it — 1in — because that is the
% conventional page and is unambiguously in spec. Anything from 0.5in up is
% equally permitted, so a filer who needs more text per page may narrow them.
\thesissetgeometry{left=1.5in, right=1in, top=1in, bottom=1in}

% "Line spacing must also be at least 18 points (1.5 lines), except for
% footnotes and long quoted passages."
%
% A FLOOR, and this profile sits on it rather than above it: 1.5 is the figure
% Guarini actually names. Double spacing also satisfies "at least 18 points"
% and is equally in spec — \thesissetspacing{double} is a one-line change — but
% onehalf is what the requirement says, and it is what the class's `onehalf'
% keyword produces.
\thesissetspacing{onehalf}

% --- Two rules this file cannot express, recorded so they are not lost --------
%
% A BLANK PAGE FOLLOWS THE TITLE PAGE, and it is neither counted nor numbered:
% "Each page in the thesis, except the title page and the blank page following
% the title page, should be assigned a number. ... The title page counts as page
% i but the number does not appear. The blank page is not counted or numbered."
% A profile owns no hook for that leaf, so a Dartmouth filer must emit it
% themselves after \thesistitlepage.
%
% MINIMUM TYPE SIZE: "Text must be at least 11-point font." A floor, not a
% face, so there is nothing to set — but a document that shrinks below 11pt is
% out of spec.
%
% Page numbers are "placed at the center bottom", which is the class's default.

% --- Accessibility -----------------------------------------------------------
% Recorded as an absence, and the absence was looked for. The whole of the
% May 2025 guidelines was read and contains none of "accessib", "alt text",
% "WCAG", "tagged", "PDF/A" or "screen reader". The HTML page that would have
% been the other place to look is the one that now 404s.
\thesisaccessibilityrequirement{Not published}
	{The Guarini School publishes no accessibility requirement for a filed
	 thesis or dissertation. Checked against the whole of the May 2025 thesis
	 guidelines on 2026-09-13. Note that the Guarini submission page that would
	 have been the second place to look was returning "Page Not Found" on that
	 date, so this is an absence in the guidelines rather than across the whole
	 site.}

% --- Profile-local field ------------------------------------------------------
% The Dean of the Guarini School signs every Dartmouth title page, so the name
% belongs to the layout rather than to the student's metadata.
%
% Not hardcoded: the May 2025 specimen prints "F. Jon Kull, Ph.D.", which is one
% copy-paste away for anyone who confirms it is still current, but an
% office-holder's name baked into a profile rots silently and a stale name over
% a signature line is a filing error that looks like a correct page.
\newcommand{\thesis@dc@dean}{[SET THE GUARINI DEAN, WITH DEGREE]}
\newcommand{\dartmouthdean}[1]{\renewcommand{\thesis@dc@dean}{#1}}

% --- Title page --------------------------------------------------------------
% The "Title Page Format for all Degrees EXCEPT Engineering Sciences and MALS"
% specimen, read from the rendered page. Its body, centred:
%
%   THE TITLE OF THE THESIS            (bold)
%   A Thesis
%   Submitted to the Faculty
%   in partial fulfillment of the requirements for the
%   degree of
%   <Master of (Fine) Arts or Master of Science or Doctor of Philosophy>
%   in
%   <Degree Major>
%   by Your Name
%   Guarini School of Graduate and Advanced Studies
%   Dartmouth College
%   Hanover, New Hampshire
%   Month and Year of Defense
%
% then a right-aligned signature block headed "Examining Committee:", with a
% rule above each name and "(chair)" before the first, and finally a
% bottom-left block: a rule, the Dean's name and degree, and "Dean of the
% Guarini School of Graduate and Advanced Studies".
%
% THE SIGNATURES GO ON THIS PAGE. Guarini states it plainly — all signatures
% must be on one title page, and electronic signatures are allowed, ideally as a
% high-resolution image of an actual signature. That is why this profile draws
% real rules here and declares no separate approval page below.
%
% TWO OTHER TITLE-PAGE FORMATS EXIST in the same PDF and are NOT encoded here:
% Engineering Sciences (which adds a "Thayer School of Engineering" line above
% the Guarini line) and MALS. A filer in either should override
% \thesistitlepage; the difference is one line for Engineering Sciences.
%
% \thesis@program supplies the degree major, which Guarini requires to be drawn
% from a published list — "List of approved majors on following page" — so set
% \gradprogram to a name on that list rather than to a research area.
\renewcommand{\thesistitlepage}{%
	\loadgeometry{nopagenumber}%
	\begin{titlepage}%
		% "The title page counts as page i but the number does not appear."
		% THE WHOLE PAGE MUST FIT ONE TEXT BLOCK. It carries a great deal —
		% thirteen centred lines, a four-signature committee block and the
		% Dean's block — and the first arrangement of it pushed the Dean onto a
		% second page, which would have put a signature line on a numbered page
		% that Guarini's pagination rules say is the blank leaf. The skips below
		% are therefore deliberately tight; with five committee signatures the
		% page will start to crowd, and the fix is to reduce them further rather
		% than to let it spill.
		\thispagestyle{empty}\singlespacing
		{\centering
			\thesis@tagtitle{Title}{{\bfseries\MakeUppercase{\@title}}}\par
			\vspace{1.5\baselineskip}
			A \thesis@DocNoun\par
			Submitted to the Faculty\par
			in partial fulfillment of the requirements for the\par
			degree of\par
			\vspace{0.75\baselineskip}
			\thesis@degree\par
			\vspace{0.75\baselineskip}
			in\par
			\vspace{0.75\baselineskip}
			\thesis@program\par
			\vspace{0.75\baselineskip}
			by \@author\par
			\vspace{0.75\baselineskip}
			Guarini School of Graduate and Advanced Studies\par
			Dartmouth College\par
			Hanover, New Hampshire\par
			\vspace{0.75\baselineskip}
			\thesis@date\par
		\par}
		\vspace{1.25\baselineskip}
		% Examining committee, right-aligned: a rule to sign on, then the name
		% beneath it. The class default \thesis@memberline puts a 3in rule and
		% then "Name, Role"; Guarini wants the role only on the chair, and as a
		% prefix rather than a suffix.
		\hfill
		\begin{minipage}{0.45\textwidth}
			\raggedright
			Examining Committee:\par
			\vspace{0.75\baselineskip}
			\begingroup
			\renewcommand{\thesis@memberline}[2]{%
				\vspace{1.5\baselineskip}%
				\rule{\linewidth}{0.4pt}\par
				##1\par
			}%
			\vspace{1.5\baselineskip}%
			\rule{\linewidth}{0.4pt}\par
			(chair) \thesis@advisor\par
			\thesis@committee
			\endgroup
		\end{minipage}%
		\par
		\vspace{2\baselineskip}
		% The Dean's block, bottom left. Wider than the committee block on
		% purpose: at 0.45\textwidth the title line wrapped as "Dean of the
		% Guarini School of / Graduate and Advanced Studies", where the specimen
		% keeps it on one line. The signature rule stays the committee's width
		% rather than the block's, which is also what the specimen shows.
		\noindent
		\begin{minipage}{0.75\textwidth}
			\raggedright
			\rule{0.45\textwidth}{0.4pt}\par
			\thesis@dc@dean\par
			Dean of the Guarini School of Graduate and Advanced Studies\par
		\end{minipage}%
		\par

	\end{titlepage}%
	\loadgeometry{normal}%
}

% --- Committee approval page --------------------------------------------------
% There is no separate one, and as at Utah State this is not an inference from
% silence: the signatures are demonstrably ON the title page above, over real
% rules, and Guarini requires them to be in exactly one place — all signatures
% on one title page.
%
% So \thesisnoapprovalpage here means "the approvals are already on the title
% page", NOT "this school collects no signatures". Dartmouth does collect them;
% it accepts electronic ones, ideally a high-resolution image of an actual
% signature, and the separate Defense Outcome and Degree Completion Form that
% travels to the Dean's office may also be signed digitally.
\thesisnoapprovalpage
