% UIC PUBLISHES NO PROSE MARGIN OR SPACING RULE, and that is a finding, not a
% gap in the search. The Manual's FORMATTING REQUIREMENTS section covers
% pagination, the title page, the preliminary pages and accessibility, and never
% states a margin, a line spacing or a body font size. It is explicit about why:
% the Graduate College "has delegated the responsibility for quality control of
% content, most aspects of format, choice of style ... to the student's academic
% program", and its own review is a listed, closed set — the title page, the
% preliminary pages, "Consistency of formatting in the body of the thesis", and
% accessibility. Body margins are nowhere in it.
%
% What the Manual does say is "Please use the available Word template and LaTeX
% template ... to help format your work", and both of those agree: the LaTeX
% template declares \geometry{margin=1in} and \doublespacing, and the Word
% sample title page is set at 1in left, right and bottom. The figures below are
% therefore TEMPLATE evidence under RESEARCHING.md's source rule (2), not a
% quoted requirement. They are the right default and they are not a rule the
% Graduate College will check.

\thesisinstitution{University of Illinois Chicago}

% \geometry{margin=1in} in the UIC LaTeX template; 1in left/right/bottom in the
% Word sample title page (its top margin is 2in, which is handled on the title
% page itself, below). No prose source — see the header.
\thesissetgeometry{left=1in, right=1in, top=1in, bottom=1in}

% \doublespacing in the UIC LaTeX template, whose comment offers the
% alternatives: "%other options are \onehalfspacing or \singlespacing". No prose
% source. \thesissetchapteropening is NOT called: no chapter-opening exception
% appears in any of the four sources.
\thesissetspacing{double}

% --- Accessibility -----------------------------------------------------------
% UIC is among the most demanding of the schools profiled so far, and its rule
% is unconditional and in force now — no future effective date is given, and the
% ADA Title II citation carries no compliance date at all. Nothing here should be
% reconciled against a neighbouring profile's date.
%
% Tagging: "All UIC theses and dissertations must comply with Web Content
% Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1 Level AA standards. To demonstrate
% compliance with WCAG 2.1 AA, all UIC thesis and dissertation submissions must
% pass an accessibility check." An untagged PDF fails that check outright.
%
% Document language: this one is an inference from a checkable rule, and it is
% flagged as such so a reviewer can overrule it. UIC does not write "set the
% document language"; it writes that the PDF "must pass 31 of 32 possible
% options in all categories of Acrobat's accessibility check. (The only item not
% required is the table summary.)" Acrobat's checker has exactly 32 items and
% Primary Language is one of them, so the language is required by name via the
% only item UIC exempts being a different one. The ePub route requires "zero (0)
% violations on all WCAG 2.0 lines and on the WCAG 2.1 A and AA lines", which
% includes 3.1.1 Language of Page. Both routes therefore require it.
%
% No PDF standard is named anywhere — no PDF/A, no PDF/UA — so
% \thesisrequirepdfstandard is NOT called.
\thesisrequiretagging
\thesisrequiredocumentlanguage
\thesisaccessibilityrequirement{Standard}
	{"All UIC theses and dissertations must comply with Web Content
	 Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1 Level AA standards", under Title II of
	 the ADA. No compliance date is given; the rule is stated as being in force.}
\thesisaccessibilityrequirement{Accessibility check}
	{Passing an automated check is a condition of acceptance: "Any work that
	 fails an accessibility check will be sent back to the author for
	 remediation. In order to have your submission approved, your work must pass
	 the accessibility check." For a PDF, "your final PDF document must pass 31
	 of 32 possible options in all categories of Acrobat's accessibility check.
	 (The only item not required is the table summary.)"}
\thesisaccessibilityrequirement{LaTeX route}
	{"Theses and dissertations created with LaTeX should be converted to both
	 PDF and ePub formats. The ePub format will be checked for accessibility
	 using the Ace by DAISY app ... your ePub document must show a total of zero
	 (0) violations on all WCAG 2.0 lines and on the WCAG 2.1 A and AA lines in
	 the Ace by DAISY Report Summary." A LaTeX filer who submits no ePub is held
	 to the PDF check instead: "your PDF version will be required to pass an
	 accessibility check."}
\thesisaccessibilityrequirement{Alt text}
	{"All informative figures must include alternative text offering a full
	 explanation of the figure."}
\thesisaccessibilityrequirement{Reading order}
	{"Document reading order must be logical (i.e., the sequence in which
	 content is presented by screen readers must ensure that meaning is
	 preserved)." UIC notes this cannot be checked automatically.}
\thesisaccessibilityrequirement{Colour and contrast}
	{"Figures must not rely on color alone to convey information or distinguish
	 a visual element." Text needs a contrast ratio of at least 4.5:1 with its
	 background, or 3:1 for large text (18pt regular, 14pt bold); any graphical
	 non-textual element needs at least 3:1. A figure that cannot meet all three
	 must carry a long description or an accessible data table.}
\thesisaccessibilityrequirement{Responsibility}
	{"It is the student's responsibility to ensure accessibility of the final
	 thesis document."}

% --- Title page --------------------------------------------------------------
% "The spacing, format, and content of the title page should follow precisely
% the sample UIC thesis title page and the formatting in the Word and LaTeX
% templates." The layout below is Sample D, measured out of the .docx: a 2in top
% margin, Times New Roman 12pt throughout, the title bold, centred and
% double-spaced (its Heading 1 style), everything else single-spaced, and the
% committee block flush left with the members indented 1in. The vertical gaps
% are the sample's own paragraph spacing converted from twips (1680, 240, 1200,
% 240, 480, 960 → 84pt, 12pt, 60pt, 12pt, 24pt, 48pt).
%
% The 2in top margin is produced as \vspace*{1in} on top of the geometry's 1in,
% because the class has no per-page geometry key for it. If the geometry above
% is ever changed, that arithmetic has to change with it.
%
% WORDING, all from the Manual, quoted:
%   * "Either the word 'THESIS' (for master's theses or doctoral dissertations)
%     or the word 'DISSERTATION' (for doctoral dissertations only) must
%     appear—centered on its own line—above your degree statement." The sample
%     uses THESIS for a doctoral document; \doctype picks the word here, which
%     is in spec either way.
%   * The degree statement "must read": "Submitted as partial fulfillment of the
%     requirements for the degree of [DEGREE] in [PROGRAM] in the Graduate
%     College of the University of Illinois Chicago, [YEAR]". Its four line
%     breaks below are the sample's.
%   * "The words 'Chicago, Illinois' should appear below your degree statement
%     (centered and on their own line)."
%   * "A list of previously earned degrees should appear below your name on your
%     title page. You should not include the degree for which your thesis is
%     being submitted."
%
% TWO THINGS A FILER MUST SET, because the class's metadata model has no field
% for either:
%   * \uicpriordegrees{...} — the previously earned degrees, one per line,
%     separated by \\. Defined just below; empty by default, and an empty list
%     simply prints nothing.
%   * \graddate — UIC's statement ends in a YEAR, not a month and year, so set
%     \graddate{2027}, not \graddate{May, 2027}.
\newcommand{\thesis@uic@priordegrees}{}
\newcommand{\uicpriordegrees}[1]{\renewcommand{\thesis@uic@priordegrees}{#1}}

\renewcommand{\thesistitlepage}{%
	\loadgeometry{nopagenumber}%
	\begin{titlepage}%
		\thispagestyle{empty}\singlespacing\centering
		\vspace*{1in}% geometry's 1in + this = the sample's 2in top margin
		\begingroup\doublespacing
		\thesis@tagtitle{Title}{{\bfseries\@title}}\par
		\endgroup
		\vspace{84pt}
		BY\par
		\vspace{12pt}
		\@author\par
		\thesis@uic@priordegrees\par
		\vspace{60pt}
		\MakeUppercase{\thesis@docnoun}\par
		\vspace{12pt}
		Submitted as partial fulfillment of the requirements\\
		for the degree of \thesis@degree{} in \thesis@program\\
		in the Graduate College of the\\
		University of Illinois Chicago, \thesis@date\par
		\vspace{24pt}
		Chicago, Illinois\par
		\vspace{48pt}
		% "The format of the title page for an ETD should include the names and
		% roles of the defense committee. The chair and advisor should be
		% specified as well as department (if from UIC) or institution (if from
		% outside UIC) of any outside members." The sample prints those as
		% "Name, Chair" / "Name, Advisor" / "Name" / "Name, Psychology" /
		% "Name, University of South Michigan" — a role OR an affiliation in the
		% same slot, with no signature rule, so the class's default member line
		% is replaced for this page.
		\begingroup
		\renewcommand{\thesis@memberline}[2]{%
			##1\ifx\relax##2\relax\else, ##2\fi\par
		}%
		\raggedright
		Defense Committee:\par
		\begingroup\leftskip=1in\relax
		\thesis@committee
		\endgroup
		\endgroup
		\vfill
	\end{titlepage}%
	\loadgeometry{normal}%
}

% --- Committee approval page --------------------------------------------------
% UIC prescribes none. The committee is named on the title page instead, and
% approval travels on a separate form: "At the defense, the Examination Report
% is signed by the committee. The completed and endorsed Examination Report must
% be sent by the student's program to the Graduate College within two business
% days of the defense." The Manual's list of what the Graduate College checks in
% the submitted document names the title page, the preliminary pages, the body,
% the vita and the abstract — and separately names the Examination Report as a
% document the PROGRAM sends, not a page in the thesis.
%
% So signatures are still collected, on paper or electronically, but never in
% the filed manuscript.
\thesisnoapprovalpage
