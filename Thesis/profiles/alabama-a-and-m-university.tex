% THE SOURCE IS OLD, AND THAT IS THE FIRST THING TO KNOW ABOUT THIS FILE. The
% title page of the guidelines reads "2012 - 2013" and its copyright line "June
% 2012"; the PDF was last modified in August 2017. It is nonetheless the current
% published document: it is the only formatting guide on the Graduate School's
% site, and the graduate-admissions pages link no newer edition. Every figure
% below is quoted from it, and every one of them should be re-checked with the
% School of Graduate Studies before a real filing — some of its rules (25-pound
% 100% cotton paper, "holes or perforations are not permitted") plainly predate
% electronic submission.
%
% VERIFIED and set here: geometry, spacing, the chapter-opening margin, the
% title page and the Certificate of Approval.
% NOT set: the typeface (see below).
%
% REQUIREMENTS THIS CLASS CANNOT ENCODE, all stated by the guidelines:
%   * Pagination. The order table reads: Fly Page (uncounted, unnumbered), Title
%     Page (counted, unnumbered), Certificate of Approval (counted, small roman
%     "ii"), Copyright Page ("iii"), and so on, with the text restarting at
%     Arabic 1. The page number sits "one inch above the bottom edge". Neither
%     the numbering scheme nor the number's position is a profile field, and the
%     blank fly pages are the document's business.
%   * "The text may be either 'justified' or 'left justified' as long as it is
%     used consistently."

% The ampersand is escaped: this value is typeset, not just stored.
\thesisinstitution{Alabama A\&M University}

% "The margins for each page of the thesis or dissertation (preliminary pages,
% text, references) -- must meet the following specifications, measured from the
% edge of the paper to the text: left 1 1/2 inch, right 1 inch, top 1 inch,
% bottom 1 inch. This margin is inviolable."
\thesissetgeometry{left=1.5in, right=1in, top=1in, bottom=1in}

% "Text is double-spaced throughout the thesis or dissertation" -- the annotation
% on the Graduate School's own sample chapter page (Appendix N). The prose adds
% that references are "double-spaced between entries, single-spaced within each
% entry", which is a local choice in the bibliography.
\thesissetspacing{double}

% "The chapter heading and title start 2 inches below the top of the page", and
% again in prose: "Each major subdivision of the thesis or dissertation begins on
% a new page with the heading two inches from the top of the page and centered
% over the text." The same 2in figure is given separately for TABLE OF CONTENTS,
% LIST OF TABLES, LIST OF FIGURES, ACKNOWLEDGMENTS and REFERENCES, which is
% exactly the set this hook covers.
\thesissetchapteropening{2in}

% The type rule is soft and its examples are dated: "A 12 point book type font
% such as Helvetica, Palatino, or Times Roman should be used consistently
% throughout the document." Nothing is forced here; a candidate who wants one of
% those three selects it in their own preamble.

% --- Accessibility -----------------------------------------------------------
% Searched all 66 pages for "accessib", "WCAG", "tagged" and "alt text": no
% match. The document predates every accessibility rule now common at filing.
\thesisaccessibilityrequirement{Not published}
	{The School of Graduate Studies publishes no accessibility requirement for a
	 filed thesis or dissertation. Its guidelines date from 2012 and predate the
	 practice; treat the silence as its age, not as a considered position.}

% --- Title page --------------------------------------------------------------
% "The wording and the format of the title page must be exactly as shown in
% Appendix D" -- so this is reproduced from that specimen, and it is the one page
% in this file whose wording is mandated word for word.
%
% Appendix D writes the programme as "in the Department of Plant and Soil
% Science". A candidate whose programme is not a department should override this
% page rather than let \thesis@program be prefixed with "the Department of".
%
% "Although the title page is counted, a number is not placed on the title page
% itself."
\renewcommand{\thesistitlepage}{%
	\loadgeometry{nopagenumber}%
	\begin{titlepage}%
		\thispagestyle{empty}\singlespacing\centering
		\vspace*{\fill}
		\thesis@tagtitle{Title}{\MakeUppercase{\@title}}\par
		\vspace{3\baselineskip}
		by\par
		\vspace{2\baselineskip}
		\MakeUppercase{\@author}\par
		\vspace{3\baselineskip}
		A \MakeUppercase{\thesis@DocNoun}\par
		\vspace{3\baselineskip}
		Submitted in partial fulfillment of the requirements\\
		for the degree of \thesis@degree\\
		in the Department of \thesis@program\\
		in the School of Graduate Studies\par
		\vspace{3\baselineskip}
		Alabama A\&M University\\
		Normal, Alabama 35762\par
		\vspace{3\baselineskip}
		\thesis@date\par
		\vspace*{\fill}
	\end{titlepage}%
	\loadgeometry{normal}%
}

% --- Certificate of Approval --------------------------------------------------
% Alabama A&M DOES require an approval page inside the document, and it is
% signed: "a certificate of approval must be signed by each committee member and
% included in the thesis. (Approval sheets must contain the original signatures
% of the Advisory Committee.) The number of signature lines is equal to the
% number of members of the Advisory Committee."
%
% Reproduced from the Appendix E specimen: a short justified paragraph, then one
% blank rule per committee member, then a rule for the Dean of the Graduate
% School and one for the date. The specimen types NO names -- the rules are
% blank, to be signed -- and labels only the advisor's line, to the right of the
% rule.
%
% ONE DELIBERATE DEVIATION: this page labels EVERY member's rule with that
% member's role, not just the advisor's. The specimen shows "Major Advisor"
% alone, but labelling the rest cannot mislead a signer and the page would
% otherwise discard information the document already carries. Revert to a bare
% rule if the Graduate Office objects.
\renewcommand{\thesisapprovalpage}{%
	\clearpage\thispagestyle{empty}%
	\loadgeometry{nopagenumber}\singlespacing
	\thesis@tagtitle{H1}{{\color{white}\tiny Certificate of Approval}}\par
	\vspace*{\fill}
	\begin{minipage}{\textwidth}
		\setlength{\parindent}{2em}%
		Submitted by \MakeUppercase{\@author} in partial fulfillment of the
		requirements for the degree of \MakeUppercase{\thesis@degree}
		specializing in \MakeUppercase{\thesis@program}.\par
		Accepted on behalf of the Faculty of the Graduate School by the
		\thesis@DocNoun{} Committee:\par
	\end{minipage}\par
	\vspace{3\baselineskip}
	% One blank signature rule per committee member, indented right of centre as
	% in the specimen, with the role to the right of the rule.
	% The label sits in its own \parbox: a long role ("Graduate School
	% Representative") otherwise wraps back to the left margin and lands under
	% the wrong rule.
	\begingroup
	\newcommand{\thesis@aamu@sigline}[2]{%
		\noindent\hspace*{##1}%
		\parbox[t]{2.3in}{\rule{2.3in}{0.4pt}}%
		\hspace{0.5em}%
		\parbox[t]{\dimexpr\textwidth-##1-2.3in-0.5em\relax}%
			{\raggedright\small\strut ##2}\par
		\vspace{2\baselineskip}%
	}%
	\renewcommand{\thesis@memberline}[2]{%
		\thesis@aamu@sigline{1.7in}{##2}%
	}%
	\thesis@committee
	\vspace{\baselineskip}
	\thesis@aamu@sigline{1in}{Dean of the Graduate School}%
	\thesis@aamu@sigline{1in}{Date}%
	\endgroup
	\vspace*{\fill}
	\loadgeometry{normal}\doublespacing\clearpage
}
