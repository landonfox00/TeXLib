In this course, generative artificial intelligence and large-language-model
tools (ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and similar) may not be used to produce work you
submit for credit. The purpose of the assignments is the thinking you do in
completing them, which is not something a tool can do on your behalf. Submitting
AI-generated work will be treated as a form of cheating.
