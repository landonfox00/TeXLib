\syllabussection{Office Hours}

scheduling notes here.

turnaround students can expect for messages.

\syllabussection{Course Description}
A short paragraph describing the course. The following topics will be covered:
\begin{itemize}
	\item First topic
	\item Second topic
	\item Third topic
\end{itemize}

\syllabussection{Student Learning Outcomes}
Upon completion of this course, students will be able to:
\begin{enumerate}
	\item First outcome.
	\item Second outcome.
	\item Third outcome.
\end{enumerate}

\syllabussection{Prerequisites}
List the prerequisites for this course and any concurrent-enrollment
requirements.

\syllabussection{Textbook}
\textit{Title} by Author, Nth ed., Publisher. Note whether a copy is required
and how students obtain it.

\syllabussection{Grade Scale}
Course grades will be determined as follows:

\gradetables{
	Component One   & 00\% \\ \hline
	Component Two   & 00\% \\ \hline
	Component Three & 00\% \\ \hline
	Final Exam      & 00\% \\ \hline
	Extra Credit    & 00\%
}{
	A & \gradebound{90 \leq}{}      \\ \hline
	B & \gradebound{80 \leq}{< 90} \\ \hline
	C & \gradebound{70 \leq}{< 80} \\ \hline
	D & \gradebound{60 \leq}{< 70} \\ \hline
	F & \gradebound{}{< 60}
}

\syllabussection{Course Schedule}
Point students to your schedule document, or list the key dates here.
